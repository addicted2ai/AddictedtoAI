The Week In Chess 131 is dated 12 May 1997 and reports Kasparov's loss
in the final game "last night"; game six was played on 11 May. The 2009
end is dated to ChessBase's report of the Mercosur Cup, which is the
source for the score, the 2938 performance, the handheld, and the "2522
grandmaster" the program outplayed.

That report does not itself say the program finished first, so the claim
is made here rather than in the end above. The
[HIARCS crosstable for the event](https://www.hiarcs.com/games/mercosur2009/mercosur09.html)
records Pocket Fritz 4 in first place on 9½/10, undefeated, ahead of
GM Rodriguez Vila (2522), GM Valerga (2507) and GM Slipak (2469), and
names the hardware as an HTC Touch HD. The two sources disagree on the
field's strength — ChessBase calls it category seven, HIARCS Category 8 —
so neither figure is quoted as settled here.

Worth being exact about what the second end shows. Deep Blue beat the
strongest human alive; a category 7–8 field is strong grandmaster
opposition but not that. What collapsed between the two dates is the
hardware, not the standard of play: the same class of result moved from a
purpose-built machine to a device that fits in a pocket.
