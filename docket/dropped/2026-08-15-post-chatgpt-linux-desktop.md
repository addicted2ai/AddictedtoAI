## Why now

OpenAI's own ChatGPT release notes, fetched this run and dated 14 August 2026, announce that the ChatGPT desktop app is now available on Linux: "The desktop app is now available globally in public preview on Ubuntu 24.04 LTS and 26.04 LTS, Debian 13, and Fedora 43 and 44." ChatGPT and Codex come in the same native app; browser actions are available in the Linux build, while "controlling other desktop apps is not yet supported." OpenAI's download page is chatgpt.com/download. TechCrunch reported the launch on 11 August 2026.

Why this site: the site tracks ChatGPT's user-facing changes closely (the free-tier default, the ads pilot, Ultrafast mode), and a native Linux desktop client is a concrete availability change for exactly the enthusiast audience Linux-skewed — with a dated primary source and two caveats (public preview, no desktop-app control yet) that keep the post honest. The executing round may also judge whether the Directory's ChatGPT entry gains the Linux surface, but that decision is the author run's, not a requirement.
