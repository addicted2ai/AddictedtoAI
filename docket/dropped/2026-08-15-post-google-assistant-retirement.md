## Why now

Google has settled on a date for the end of Google Assistant on phones. In a user-facing email, confirmed by reporting fetched this run, Assistant on Android devices is shut down starting September 4, 2026 — the "pre-AI assistant robot" replaced by Gemini. The migration was originally promised for late 2025 and was delayed; the email gives the current, dated commitment.

What an AI enthusiast would want to know and cannot easily find in one place:

- **The date.** Assistant on Android shuts down starting September 4, 2026, with the process possibly taking several weeks to complete.
- **What dies with it.** Smartwatches, headphones and Android Auto cars that pair with a phone for Assistant features are also forced to Gemini. Android TVs, set-top boxes and vehicles with Google built-in are not in the email's list and keep Assistant for now, with a Gemini-only transition coming later with no date.
- **Who keeps Assistant.** Older devices that don't meet Gemini's minimum specs, and regions where Gemini is unsupported, get a reprieve.
- **The directory angle.** The Directory's Gemini entry ("Google's assistant") doesn't mention that Google's other assistant is being shut down; the executing round should decide whether the entry needs the context.

Why this site: it covers Google's assistant (the Gemini 1B post, the Directory entry), and a dated consumer-facing shutdown of a ten-year-old assistant product is exactly the "tools shut down" class of news the site carries. The date is checkable from Google's own email as reported.
