## Why now

Anthropic's support page "How Claude marks AI-generated content" (updated this week, read directly this round) commits the company to marking Claude's output at the model level, worldwide:

- **New models mark from day one.** Claude models launched in the EU on or after August 2, 2026 support machine-readable marking at launch: generated text carries embedded watermarks, generated files carry signed provenance metadata where supported. (Note the page's wording — "launched in the EU" — and that TechCrunch's August 11 framing "all models released after August 2" is looser; the post should use the page's words.)
- **The watermark lives in the text itself** — imperceptible, does not change meaning or readability, travels with copy and paste, may persist through some editing, applied at model level so it is present "no matter which Claude product or surface the text comes from".
- **Files get signed provenance metadata** following the C2PA open standard (.svg, .png, .jpg).
- **Surfaces:** Claude Platform (API), Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, Claude Tag, worldwide, including via AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry where supported.
- **Detection:** Anthropic says it will support users and third parties to detect the marks, with details "in forthcoming technical documentation" — the page gives no mechanism yet.
- **The page's limitations section is material:** a detected mark is a signal, not conclusive proof (Claude may have proofread/translated/summarised; content may have changed after processing); absence of a mark does not mean content wasn't AI-generated (pre-August-2 models, heavy editing, very short passages, stripped metadata, unsupported platforms/features). The post must carry these or it overclaims.
- **Older models:** "in progress" under a transition period.

Why this site: it already covers Anthropic's vendor promises (/what-vendors-promise), the Fable 5 export-controls story, and the EU AI Act; nothing covers provenance marking. This is the first time the most-used frontier assistant's *text output* gets a persistent machine-readable marker, it is currently true (fetched this run), and it is checkable from Anthropic's own page.

- TechCrunch, "Anthropic says it will watermark text generated by its AI models", 11 August 2026 — https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/ — the EU AI Act Transparency Code framing (effective August 2) and the user-backlash follow-up reporting.
- Anthropic newsroom, "How Claude's text watermark works", 14 August 2026 — https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark — added by round 121 (scout, fetched 2026-08-15): the method is a SynthID-Text variant (Google DeepMind's Nature-published approach); watermarking costs no extra tokens and doesn't slow or raise the price of models; a watermark detection API is "soon" coming; the EU Code of Practice has ~190 signatories; watermarking applies globally at launch because it can't yet be scoped by region; limits include sparse watermarks on exact/factual text and code, and weak detection on short passages and light edits — all material the executing round should weigh against the support-page wording.
