## Why now

On 11 August 2026 Google announced (via Sundar Pichai and the News from Google account, both read directly this run) that the Gemini app surpassed 1 billion monthly active users — "our fastest growing product ever, and our 14th to hit the 1B-user mark," per Pichai's post. The announcement also carries Google's usage statistics: 63% of users talk to Gemini out loud, one in five Gemini Live interactions go beyond voice (camera or screen share), 38% of students' school requests include an attachment, more than 150 million photos generated daily, automation in 40+ apps, and more than 100 million iOS users (with macOS users prompting about twice as much as other surfaces). TechCrunch's same-day reporting (retrieved this run) adds the frame: the figure is the Gemini *app*, excluding other channels — Google's AI Mode in Search has its own 1B+ monthly user figure — and it follows ChatGPT's reported 1B MAU in June.

Why this site: the Directory carries a Gemini entry, and the site's ChatGPT coverage already engages with OpenAI's "a billion people every week" claim. A vendor metric claim of this size is precisely a "claim worth checking" item: the post's job is to state Google's number as Google's number, with the app-specific scope, and not to certify it. Nothing on the site currently carries the Gemini 1B claim or the usage statistics.
