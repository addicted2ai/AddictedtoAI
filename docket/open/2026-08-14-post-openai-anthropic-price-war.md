## Why now

On 14 August 2026 the Financial Times (via Ars Technica) reported that OpenAI and Anthropic are in a price war for cost-conscious customers switching to cheaper Chinese models. The two vendor-side facts are verified this run against both companies' own pricing pages:

- **Anthropic's Opus 5 is priced at half of Fable 5.** Anthropic's pricing page, fetched this run, lists Claude Opus 5 at $5 / MTok input and $25 / MTok output — exactly half of Claude Fable 5's $10 / $50. (Opus 5 launched 24 July, per Anthropic's newsroom, but the FT piece — 14 August — is the first time this is framed as a war move, and the pricing page is the current, checkable evidence.)
- **Anthropic cancelled the scheduled 1 September Sonnet 5 price rise.** The pricing page's note, fetched this run, says Sonnet 5's $2/$10 launch pricing "is now the standard price. The previously scheduled increase to $3/$15 per million input/output tokens on September 1, 2026 will not occur." The FT piece dates the cancellation to this week.
- **OpenAI's side is the 30 July Luna cut**, already covered by this site's published gpt-5-6-price-drop post — the executing round must connect to that post rather than re-report the cut as new. The FT numbers ($1 → $0.20 input, $6 → $1.20 output) match OpenAI's pricing page, fetched this run: gpt-5.6-luna at $0.20 / $1.20.

The context an AI enthusiast wants: the war is framed as a response to Chinese open models (DeepSeek's V4 Flash, Moonshot's Kimi K3), mid-tier prices have fallen roughly 25% since mid-July per Silicon Data's token price index (a third-party number — attribute it, don't verify it), and the FT reports Anthropic/OpenAI shifting enterprise customers toward usage-based billing. Anthropic and OpenAI declined to comment; a person close to Anthropic said Opus 5's below-flagship pricing is just "how the family of models is built."
