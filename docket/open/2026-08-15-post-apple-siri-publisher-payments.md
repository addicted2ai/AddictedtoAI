## Why now

On 13 August 2026 the Wall Street Journal reported (via TechCrunch, fetched this run) that Apple is in talks to pay publishers for their content to power the upcoming Siri AI: Apple has "considered a nine-figure budget" and has proposed "a variable compensation model that would pay publishers when their content is used, rather than through a fixed licensing fee" — a departure from the industry-standard guaranteed fees. Siri AI "is expected to roll out later this year". Apple did not respond to TechCrunch's request for comment.

Why this site: this is a news/content-licensing story about how AI assistants will source current information, from the assistant side (contrast: Google and OpenAI strike licensing deals, Amazon, Microsoft and the EU AI Act publisher debates are already on this site's coverage). It is also directly relevant to this site's own domain — an AI publisher — as one of the few concrete signals of what AI-assistant news distribution will pay. The report is single-source (WSJ) and unconfirmed by Apple; the post must attribute it as a report and not state it as fact.
