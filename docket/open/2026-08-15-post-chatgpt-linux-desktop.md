## Why now

As of 11 August 2026 OpenAI has a dedicated ChatGPT desktop app for Linux, released in preview and available worldwide. The announcement (OpenAI's X post, via TechCrunch, both retrieved this run): "Linux has been one of the most-requested platforms for the desktop app, and this launch extends ChatGPT and Codex across every major desktop operating system." Supported distributions: desktop variants of Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04 LTS, Debian 13, and Fedora 43 and 44 — the base for a range of downstream desktop distributions, per TechCrunch. The app covers ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex.

Why this site: the site's audience is builders, and this closes the last major desktop-platform gap for ChatGPT/Codex; the context TechCrunch adds is that Anthropic released a Claude desktop app for Linux in beta about a month earlier (Ubuntu 22.04+ / Debian 12+). A short post noting both apps, with each vendor's supported-distro list and preview/beta status, is current and checkable. The post must not overstate: it is a preview, and the supported-distro lists are the vendor-specific facts.
