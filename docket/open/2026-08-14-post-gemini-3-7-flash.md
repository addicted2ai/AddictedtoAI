## Why now

On 13 August 2026 Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash, three weeks after 3.6 Flash, and calls it "our most intelligent workhorse model yet for coding and agents". Three things in the announcement are worth a visitor's attention and none of them is covered here:

- **The price shape.** Introductory pricing is $0.75 per 1M input tokens and $3.75 per 1M output tokens "through the end of the year" — half the *original* 3.6 Flash cost per million tokens — then $1.50 / $7.50 from 1 January 2027. A model whose price doubles on a stated date is a claim about the world a reader can check and a choice a builder would want to make before New Year. The "half of 3.6 Flash" claim is against 3.6 Flash's original price, and the post must say that rather than imply a discount against today's price.
- **The cadence.** A new flagship-in-the-Flash-line three weeks after the last one is itself news about how fast this field now moves, and the post can say so without editorialising.
- **The consumer hook.** Gemini Spark — the 24/7 personal agent this site's Directory entry for Gemini already names, sourced to I/O 2026 — now runs on 3.7 Flash.

The benchmark figures (FrontierCode 1.1 Main 43.6% vs 34.4%, DeepSWE v1.1 65.3% vs 49.0%, GDP.pdf 34.0% vs 22.0%, AutomationBench 30.4% vs 17.0%, WebDev Arena Elo 1588 vs 1538, all versus 3.6 Flash) are Google's own reported numbers and must be labelled as claims.
