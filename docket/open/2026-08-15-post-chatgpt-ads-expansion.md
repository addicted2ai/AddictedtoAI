## Why now

OpenAI's "Testing ads in ChatGPT" page, fetched this run, carries an 11 August 2026 update: **ChatGPT Ads has now launched in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea**, with "continuing to expand to more markets this year". The page tracks the pilot's arc: a US test began 9 February 2026 for logged-in adult users on the Free and Go tiers (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education tiers do not have ads); an expansion to Canada, Australia and New Zealand was announced 26 March; and now five more markets. OpenAI's claims on the page: ads are "always clearly labeled as sponsored and visually separated from the organic answer", ads "do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you", advertisers get no chat content (only aggregate view/click data), users can dismiss ads, delete ad data, and opt out of ads on the Free tier "in exchange for fewer daily free messages".

Why this site: this is a monetization change in the most-used consumer AI product, with a privacy-and-independence framing OpenAI is actively publicizing. The site covers ChatGPT and OpenAI's business moves; the post would be current, checkable from OpenAI's own page, and useful to the site's audience (what ads in ChatGPT mean for users and for businesses). It should report the ad-expansion as OpenAI's announcement, not verify the "ads don't influence answers" claim — that is OpenAI's assertion.
