## Why now

On 13 August 2026 OpenAI announced Ultrafast, a new service tier that runs GPT-5.6 Sol up to 14x faster than Standard processing, "launching first in the OpenAI API". The framing matters for this site's audience of practitioners: OpenAI's own words are that real-time speed previously "typically meant choosing a smaller or more specialized model", and Ultrafast is the first time the *most* intelligent model gets the low-latency tier — "more useful work per second". It is powered by OpenAI's existing partnership with Cerebras (up to 750 output tokens per second), available today in a limited preview to a select group of customers, with access to expand "as capacity grows" (sign-up form live).

Why this site: the Directory's OpenAI entry and the site's ChatGPT coverage track the vendor's product line, and the site has covered the model family (gpt-5.6-luna pricing in the price-war item filed this week). Ultrafast is the first Cerebras-powered tier for OpenAI's frontier model — a concrete, checkable product fact announced this week that the site does not cover. The post should report OpenAI's claims as claims (the 14x figure, the 750 tokens-per-second figure, the preview status, the customer testimonials from Jane Street, Podium, Basis and Rogo are OpenAI-selected quotes, not independent verification), and should not certify latency or speed it cannot measure.
