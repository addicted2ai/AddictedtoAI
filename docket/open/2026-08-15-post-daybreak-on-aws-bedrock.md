## Why now

The site's published frontier-cyber post (fetched 10 August) covers the Daybreak launch — GPT-5.6-Cyber, the Blue/Red access tiers, the 95.0% / 1.5% refusal-rate numbers, CVE-2026-15903. Two things have changed since, both after that post's fetch date:

- **11 August: Daybreak models became available on Amazon Bedrock.** OpenAI's post, fetched this run, says Daybreak Blue and Red are both available in AWS through the Daybreak Access program — Blue being the general-purpose frontier models (GPT-5.6 Sol) with safeguards tailored to defensive work, Red being the purpose-trained cyber models. Eligible customers enroll in Daybreak Access and use the `bedrock-mantle` endpoint via the Amazon Bedrock console or Responses API. The availability post is dated 11 August, one day after the launch post (10 August) that introduced the purpose-trained cyber models.
- **13 August: IBM expanded its OpenAI partnership.** IBM will train and certify tens of thousands of consultants on OpenAI's technology (Codex, API, cybersecurity credentials), integrate GPT-5.6, Codex and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage, and — per TechCrunch, fetched this run — integrate OpenAI's models with IBM Autonomous Security, extending the June Daybreak Cyber Partner Program.

Why this site: it already owns this story (the frontier-cyber post), and the distribution step — frontier cyber models available inside a cloud provider's console — is the natural follow-on an enthusiast reading the post would want. The Bedrock availability is a specific, dated, checkable change on OpenAI's own page.
