## Why now

On 11 August 2026 OpenAI updated its "Testing ads in ChatGPT" post to say that ChatGPT Ads has launched in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea. The post the site should have read by now is an arc, not a day:

- 9 February 2026 — the pilot begins in the US, for logged-in adult users on the Free and Go subscription tiers; Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education get no ads.
- 26 March 2026 — OpenAI reports "encouraging" early results ("no impact on consumer trust metrics, low dismissal rates") and announces expansion to Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
- 7 May 2026 — it announces plans to expand to the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea.
- 11 August 2026 — those five markets launch.

Two things make this a story rather than a spec update. The most-used consumer AI product in the world is becoming an ad-funded platform, and nothing on this site tells a visitor that — the site covers GPT-5.6 pricing, the Directory, agent tools, and vendor promises, and has no word on where the category leader's revenue now comes from. And the post is a page of checkable claims: that ads never influence answers, that advertisers never see chats or memory, that a free-tier user can opt out in exchange for fewer daily messages, and that the ad-matching uses conversation topic plus past chats and ad interactions. Those are exactly the claims worth checking, and the primary source is OpenAI's own page.

For the executing round: the news-listing card dates the update 12 August while the page itself says "August 11, 2026" — read the page and use its dates. A post must not overcount markets from memory; the page names them in its three update blocks.
