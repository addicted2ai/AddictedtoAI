## Why now

On 13 August 2026 OpenAI previewed Ultrafast, a service tier that runs GPT-5.6 Sol at up to 14x standard processing speed, generating up to 750 output tokens per second, and says it is "powered by Cerebras". It launches first in the OpenAI API, in limited preview to a select group of customers, with a signup page for access notifications.

Two things make this more than a speed-boast release note. First, it is the speed half of the price-performance story this site already covers: the GPT-5.6 price-drop post covers what the family costs; nothing covers the other axis, the 14x speed class, and the post's own line — "until now, getting real-time speed typically meant choosing a smaller or more specialized model" — is the claim worth quoting. Second, a frontier OpenAI model served on a third party's hardware (Cerebras) is a structural change in where frontier inference runs, and the announcement says it is "the next step in our partnership with Cerebras" — a partnership history the site has never recorded.

The post must not overclaim. "14X", "750 tokens per second" and the customer quotes are all OpenAI's claims; availability is a limited preview, not general release; and no pricing is announced, so the post must not invent any.
