## Why now

Microsoft's own support pages, read directly this round, document a consolidation of its Copilot assistant apps:

- **The merge.** The consumer-facing Copilot app and the Microsoft 365 Copilot app combine into one updated app: sign-in with a personal or work/school account (or both), work and personal accounts kept separate, chat history and content moved across, standalone-app files migrated to OneDrive.
- **Retiring features, all dated 18 August 2026.** Podcasts (no longer available after August 18), Deep Research in the consumer Copilot app (retired starting August 18), Group Chat (account updates begin August 18; group chats migrate to individual one-to-one chats; other participants' prompts/artifacts/media are not accessible after migration), Copilot Labs experimental features, and the Mico character (per the retired-features FAQ).
- **What replaces what.** Microsoft points paying professional users to Researcher as the replacement for Deep Research (per TechCrunch's reporting of the support documentation).

The move is a tool being consolidated and partially shut down — squarely the "tools shipped or shut down" category this site tracks — and it is dated and checkable from Microsoft's pages. It also touches the site's Directory only at the edges: the Directory lists GitHub Copilot, the coding agent, which is not the app being merged; the post must keep the two distinct.

Three things this item recorded on 2026-08-14 did not survive re-checking,
and the post reports the current state rather than the item's:

- **The Group Chat data mechanics changed.** This item's "group chats migrate
  to individual one-to-one chats; other participants' prompts/artifacts/media
  are not accessible after migration" was accurate on 2026-08-14 and is not
  the page's current answer. The FAQ now says group chat threads, messages and
  content "will not carry forward after your account updates", and the
  migrate-as-one-to-one wording is gone from both Microsoft pages. The FAQ's
  own `updated_at` metadata reads 2026-08-18 03:09 AM. The checklist line
  above is ticked on the strength of the post carrying the *current* wording
  and the earlier wording side by side, which is more than the line asked for.
- **"Retiring features, all dated 18 August 2026" overstated it.** Podcasts,
  Deep Research and Group Chat carry that date; Copilot Labs carries none
  ("Timing may vary by account") and Mico carries an explicit denial that one
  exists ("there isn't a single date").
- **Mico is not simply retired.** Microsoft's FAQ says it "is moving to Learn
  Live" and continues there as a tutor; what ends is its appearance in
  Copilot Voice.

Also noted: the merge target is called "the Microsoft Copilot app" on the live
page, not "the Microsoft 365 Copilot app" as this item's title and evidence
line record — the rename landed after the Internet Archive's 17 August
capture. The item's title is left as filed (it was correct when written).
