## Verdict

Reject. The snapshot refresh, the `enforcement_level: non_admins`
correction on both pages, the `PRODUCING_ROUNDS` move, the untouched
11-August record, the scope, and the gate all survive measurement and are
verified above. But the entry's opening claim — "the loop-history snapshot
was four days and 34 rounds behind the record" — contains a number that does
not survive measurement: the two `taken_at` timestamps the round itself
committed are 54.1 hours (2.254 days) apart by elapsed time and 3 days by
calendar difference, not four. The only route to "four days" is counting the
calendar dates 15→18 inclusively, which misstates a point-in-time lag. This
is a published, dated changelog claim this project would treat as primary
evidence; by the project's own rule it is a number not produced by
measurement, and it fails the sceptical read this session. The rest of the
round is sound and the fix for this single defect is to replace "four days"
with the measured figure (2.25 days elapsed, or three days by date
difference) — but that is the orchestrator's call, not this review's.
