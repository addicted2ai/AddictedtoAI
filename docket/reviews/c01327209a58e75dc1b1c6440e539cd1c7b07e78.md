## Blocking finding

**The changelog's Guardrails line misattributes the publishing-quota check
to `node scripts/round.mjs check`.** The entry says: "Guardrails: `node
scripts/round.mjs check` — lint, docket validator, track scope,
production-shaped build, publishing quota, and route checks all green on port
3000." I measured what `round.mjs check` actually runs: `npm run lint`,
`check-docket.mjs`, `check-track-scope.mjs`, `npm run build`, and
`check-routes.sh` (see `scripts/round.mjs`'s `check()`). `check-publishing-quota.mjs`
is a separate script, run by no part of `round.mjs` and by no step of
`pr-checks.yml` or `loop.yml`. The quota itself does pass when run directly
("ok 10 posts; day cap 1, week cap 3"), so the substance is green — but the
sentence states that `check` verified it, and `check` does not. This is the
exact defect class this project rejects: a claim written from what the guardrail
suite was meant to cover rather than what the command measures. The fix is
small (state the quota check as its own command, e.g. "`node
scripts/round.mjs check` … and `node scripts/check-publishing-quota.mjs` …
all green"), but the entry is permanent once merged, so it blocks.

## Non-blocking findings

1. **A quotation is not verbatim.** The post renders "the barrier to
   producing this class of weapon has collapsed, and it won't come back" in
   quotation marks as the researchers' line. A Security's page says "The
   barrier that kept these weapons scarce has collapsed, and it will not come
   back." Meaning and attribution are preserved; the words are not exact. On
   a site whose whole discipline is precise attribution, a quote that
   paraphrases should drop the quotation marks or match the source.
2. **"4.5-day campaign inside Hugging Face's production infrastructure."** HF
   labels the whole 07-09 → 07-13 episode its "4.5-day campaign", but its own
   timeline says the intrusion *inside its infrastructure* ran "roughly two
   and a half days" (days 1–2 were largely the external-sandbox foothold and
   C2). Tying the 4.5 days to the HF-internal phase slightly overstates the
   on-platform window. HF's framing, but worth a half-sentence of precision.
3. **KICS/Telnyx attribution fuzz.** "KICS and the Telnyx Python SDK were in
   the same campaign" sits inside the CloudSEK paragraph ("Its account of the
   chain"), but CloudSEK's page names only Trivy, KICS and LiteLLM. The Telnyx
   fact comes from Ars, which is cited two sentences later in the same
   paragraph and listed in Sources. The sentence is defensible as "public
   reporting" but reads as CloudSEK's claim where it stands.
4. **"Still pending at both sources' last updates" (METR/Redwood).** OpenAI's
   disclosure confirms the assessment is outstanding at its 28–29 July
   updates. HF's 27 July timeline does not mention METR/Redwood at all, so
   "both sources" is loose; the substance (still pending) holds.
5. **The "98% truce for Mythos 5" figure is not in the paper's text.** The
   Anthropic page confirms n=120 episodes per model, the truce/force/
   passivity categories, the models tested (including Mythos 5), and the
   force-then-reverted-to-truce phenomenon — but the exact 98% number lives
   in the "Time to resolution" chart image, which does not survive text
   extraction. I could not confirm it, and I could not falsify it. Flagged
   rather than passed.
