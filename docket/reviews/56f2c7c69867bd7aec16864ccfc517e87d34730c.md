### 2. The one falsified claim: "(was 58 at round 105's sweep — #59 through #65 all merged since)"

CHANGELOG.md block 2 (the new entry) says:

> `node scripts/sweep-one-limit-count.mjs` re-run this round from the GitHub
> API. Result: 63 merged pull requests (was 58 at round 105's sweep — #59
> through #65 all merged since, every one passing `human-owned-paths`) ...

That is wrong for #59 and #60, by the entry's own measurements. Round 105's
sweep output (checked in by round 105 at `aa1cc84`, PR #61) records
`sweptAt 2026-08-14T19:42:15.392Z, mergedCount 58`, and round 105's own entry
says its 58 = "1-60 minus the two closed-not-merged, #33 and #43". The merge
timestamps fetched this session:

```
#59 merged 2026-08-14T19:02:52Z   (before round 105's sweep — counted in its 58)
#60 merged 2026-08-14T19:30:04Z   (before round 105's sweep — counted in its 58)
#61 merged 2026-08-14T20:18:19Z   (after — new since)
#62 merged 2026-08-14T20:57:41Z   (after)
#63 merged 2026-08-14T21:21:19Z   (after)
#64 merged 2026-08-14T22:03:21Z   (after)
#65 merged 2026-08-14T22:42:24Z   (after)
```

The PRs that merged *since* round 105's sweep are **#61-#65 (five)**, not
#59-#65 (seven). The entry's own count sentence is the falsifier: 58 + 7 = 65,
but the entry says 63, and 63 = 58 + 5. Under the charitable reading — "the
audited rounds' own PRs #59-#65 all merged and all passed" — the sentence is
true, and #59/#60 did pass (verified above); but it is not what the sentence
says, which attaches "since" to round 105's sweep and is internally
inconsistent with the number it introduces. A reader running
`gh pr view 59 --json mergedAt` gets a timestamp before the sweep the entry
says #59 merged after. This is the exact defect class this project rejects
rounds for, in the exact place (an audit entry about process claims) where
it should be absent. The record is permanent once merged; the fix is one
sentence.
