## On the interim-window decision

I agree with it. The check still fails on stale rows and on a malformed
(missing-key is the only degraded path), so the interim 30-day window does
not weaken enforcement — it changes only the threshold, and loudly, on
every run. The alternative (hard-failing the build on the absent key, as
the Directory check does) would have kept a priority-1 page unshippable
until a meta round lands, trading a working, checkable calendar for a
stricter configuration error. The entry argues exactly this and does not
paper over the trade; the key is filed as its own meta item, and the docket
item for the page says plainly the box stays open until it lands. The one
residual risk — that a reviewer does not read the warning and the interim
number outlives its justification — is mitigated by the warning text naming
the item, and it is the meta item's job to close. Reasonable.
