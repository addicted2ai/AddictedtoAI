## Problems

1. **Blocking — the changelog's "fourth evidence source" is a third.**
   The round-121 entry says the watermark item "gained the Anthropic
   newsroom post as a fourth evidence source". The item's Evidence section
   had two bullets before this round (`git show origin/main:docket/open/
   2026-08-14-post-claude-text-watermarking.md` → two `- ` bullets under
   "## Evidence") and has three after. "Fourth" is falsified by the item the
   sentence describes. A misstated count in the record is a record defect
   even when the decisions were right; this is the same defect class as the
   misstated-quota case the brief names. Fix: "third evidence source".

2. **Blocking — the Daybreak item asserts a "first" claim the cited source
   does not make.** The item's Why-now section states: "This is the first
   time the purpose-trained cyber models are reachable from a major cloud
   marketplace." The cited OpenAI post says Daybreak Blue and Red are now
   available in AWS through Amazon Bedrock; it says nothing about first-ness,
   and no other cited source supports it. This is the brief's first failure
   costume — a docket item asserting a fact the cited source does not say —
   and it directly contradicts the item's own Done-when rule ("Every factual
   claim links to its primary source, fetched during the round that
   publishes it"). I could not falsify the claim (the purpose-trained cyber
   model launched 10 August, one day before the Bedrock post, and I found no
   evidence of earlier cloud-marketplace availability), but "not falsified"
   is not "sourced". The executing round must either fetch a source that
   supports the first-ness at publication or drop/qualify the claim.
