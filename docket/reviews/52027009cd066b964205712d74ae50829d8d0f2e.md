   Foundry model retirement schedule, fetched by me this run (raw HTML,
   `curl -sL .../model-retirement-schedule`), lists **three** GA Llama rows:

     Llama-4-Scout-17B-16E-Instruct) listed as generally available"
   - `app/what-vendors-promise/page.js` (footnote, ~line 270): "four more GA"
   - `app/lib/retirement-commitments.js` (Meta row sentenceMore, line 124):
     "and four GA without dates (… the same three names …)"
