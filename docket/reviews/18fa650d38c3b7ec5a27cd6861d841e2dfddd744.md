## Problems

1. **BLOCKING — the fix commit fails the suite it was required to re-run.**
   `node scripts/round.mjs check` on head 18fa650:
   `FAIL  /what-vendors-promise: mapped to round 116 (audit), but its files
   were last touched by "fix: correct the Foundry GA count to three (round
   116 review)" (fix) — update PRODUCING_ROUNDS`; the route-checks group
   fails and the command exits non-zero. Cause: check-ai-disclosure.mjs
   walks `git log` for the route's source files (lastContentCommitSubject,
   lines 78-98) and parses the newest content commit's subject prefix as a
   track (lines 157-160); the fix commit's subject starts "fix:", which
   parses to track "fix", not the mapped round 116's "audit" (lines
   204-207). The count fix necessarily touched the route's listed files,
   so it had to be committed under a subject carrying the track (every
   precedent in this repo does: round 113's quote fixes are "maintain:
   ...", and round 115's "fix: ..." review correction touched only
   docket/open/, never app files). CI confirms the failure is real, not
   local: build-and-audit passed on heads 5202700 and 50ce9fa (runs
   31868541053, 31869073315) and **failed on 18fa650** (run 31869188982,
   step "Check non-HTML routes and the 404") with this exact line. The
   first review's instruction "fix all four places and re-run the suite"
   is unmet: a required check is red at the head, so auto-merge cannot
   land the PR. Fix: re-commit the correction with a track-prefixed
   subject (e.g. "audit: correct the Foundry GA count to three (round 116
   review)") so the last content commit on the route's files parses to
   "audit", then re-run `node scripts/round.mjs check` until green and
   push.
