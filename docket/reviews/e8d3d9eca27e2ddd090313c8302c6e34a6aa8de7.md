text state the backup deadline as a bare "23 August" with no year, anywhere
they're surfaced outside the article body.** This is the exact defect class
the review brief names by example ("a bare '23 August' without enough
context is a candidate defect"). Verified against the actually rendered
build (`npm run build` + `next start -p 3000`, curled), not just source:

- `<title>` tag (browser tab / search snippet), fetched from the live build:
  `Manus Is Splitting From Meta — Some Users Have Until 7:59 A.M. SGT On 23
  August To Back Up Their Data | AddictedtoAI` — no year anywhere in the
  string.
- Homepage "Latest from the blog" card, fetched from `/`: `<h3>Manus is
  splitting from Meta, and giving some users until 7:59 a.m. SGT on 23
  August to back up their data</h3><p>...backup window for your data closes
  7:59 a.m. SGT on 23 August — after that, it's gone...</p>` — no date or
  year rendered anywhere near it (`app/page.js` renders only
  `latestPost.title` and `latestPost.excerpt`, nothing else, in that block).
- `/blog` index "More from the blog" list, fetched: the same bare-"23
  August" title text as the sole content of the `<li>`, with no date
  (`app/blog/page.js` renders `<a href={p.path}>{p.title}</a>` only).
- JSON-LD `headline` on the post's own page is `post.title`, so structured
  data search engines read carries the same omission.

`grep -n "23 August" app/lib/posts.js app/blog/manus-meta-split/page.js`
shows the split precisely: the three in-body mentions (`page.js` lines 55,
67, 72) all say "23 August 2026"; the four in `posts.js` — `title` (line
218), `metaTitle` (line 220), and the two mentions inside `excerpt` (line
224) — never say 2026 at all (`description`, line 222, opens "On 11 August
2026" so a reader has a nearby anchor, which is why I'm not flagging it).

This is not cosmetic. The whole point of this page, per the brief and per
the post's own "Where this sits on this site" section, is a deadline a
reader can still act on. The homepage teaser and the blog index list will
keep showing "some users have until 7:59 a.m. SGT on 23 August to back up
their data" verbatim, with zero disambiguating year, for as long as this
post remains `latestPost` and remains on the "More from the blog" list —
i.e. until a newer post ships. A reader who lands on the homepage after this
year's 23 August has passed (this post is not superseded on any fixed
schedule) sees a live-sounding deadline with no way to tell it already
happened, unless they click through to the article, where "Posted
2026-08-21" sits under the H1. The site's own precedent argues this was a
slip rather than a style choice: `/blog/gemini-3-7-flash`, shipped one week
earlier, puts a forward calendar deadline in its title too ("...the rate
doubles on 1 January 2027") and does include the year there.

I'm not fixing this — per the brief, reporting only.
