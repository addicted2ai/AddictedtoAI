  Jensen Huang's blog (point 5), not in the press release (point 4), where a
  full-text search of this run's fetch finds none of its distinctive phrases.
  The changelog entry itself is accurate on this point — it attributes the
  $500B mobilization figure to the press release (which does say it) and the
  qualifiers to the sources generally — so only the item needs the fix: move
  the sentence and the "clarifications" mention to the Huang-blog bullet, and
  adjust the "What the press release says it is not" heading (the release
  does contain the subject-to-final-agreements sentence). Left as written,
  a future author run executing the item's own "Every factual claim links to
  its primary source" checklist could quote the sentence under the wrong
  source in a published post.
