## Why this is still blocking

The round's own standard — in its own changelog — is that the ceiling rule
makes the queue stop growing, and that the third review's track-move finding
is closed: "the scale-out version is caught by build's own bound." The
precondition of that sentence is false. Three of the six tracks have no
`queue_budget`, so their head counts are not bounded at all, and the gate
skips them (`budget == null -> continue`). The scale-out track-move I walked
in the third review works unchanged with an unbudgeted destination: I grew
the queue 60 -> 90 in one committed diff with every check green, and the same
diff is green every round thereafter. The fix narrowed the attack to
budgeted destinations; the mechanism survives in full on the tracks the gate
does not bound. This is the same class as the three previous findings — the
gate tests per-track totals the head controls through labels (`blocked-on`
before, `track:` now), and the head still supplies more than one fact about
its own queue — and it is the fourth time the gate's headline promise is
false on a committed branch. Everything else in this revision is verified and
good: the arithmetic, the invariant, the boundary, the controls, the record,
the guard. The defect is narrow and the fix it needs is the one the round's
own invariant implies: a head-derived fact that can move items out of a
gated track's count must not exist, or the unbudgeted tracks must be bounded
too. I tried eleven attacks plus the four controls; the first ten found
nothing and are reported plainly above. This one works, and it is blocking.
