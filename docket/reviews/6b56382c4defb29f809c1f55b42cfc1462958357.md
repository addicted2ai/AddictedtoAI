## Blocking finding — item 4 of the entry contradicts the code, the check,
and the entry's own parenthetical

The entry's item 4 ("The route registration, in all the places") says:

    PRODUCING_ROUNDS (round 113, by construction — this round built it)

That is false in every direction I measured:

- `app/lib/page-origins.js:222` maps `"/loop-history": 112`, not 113.
- The entry's own opening paragraph and parenthetical say the round is 112
  and that "The mapping and this entry follow the code."
- `node scripts/check-ai-disclosure.mjs` prints, on this branch:
  `ok    /loop-history: producing round 112 (build), last commit
  "loop/build/publish the loop's failure rate at /loop-history, derived
  from a committed snapshot"` and exits 0.
- `scripts/check-review-artifact.mjs` reads the producing round the same way.

The sentence describes the map as carrying 113 "by construction — this
round built it"; the round that built it is the round this entry records,
112. The permanent record therefore contradicts the code it describes, and
contradicts itself (the parenthetical asserting the entry follows the code
is false for this sentence). Per the review contract, a falsified claim in
a changelog entry is blocking on its own: the entry is permanent once
merged. This is not the brief's error restated — commit 90d6fa9 corrected
the opening paragraph and the map from 113 to 112 and left this sentence
behind.
