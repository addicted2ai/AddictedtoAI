## The blocking finding

**The round's "nothing is covered" claim is falsified for the Gemini
milestone — by the site's own Directory.**

- `2026-08-14-post-gemini-billion-users.md` asserts: "Nothing on the site
  currently carries the Gemini 1B claim or the usage statistics."
- `app/lib/tool-categories.js:22` (present on `origin/main` before this
  branch, so visible to the round) reads: "Google's assistant — past 1
  billion monthly users, with Gemini Spark, a 24/7 personal agent that acts
  on your behalf." That Directory line was added by round 98 (PR #49) and,
  per its review and the done item `docket/done/2026-08-10-directory-missing-gemini.md`,
  was verified the same day against the very 11 August announcement this
  item is about. The changelog entry's blanket "The week's outward changes,
  none covered on the site" is likewise not true for the Gemini item: the
  site carries the 1B milestone claim already.
- The item is internally contradictory: it says "the Directory carries a
  Gemini entry" in one sentence and "Nothing on the site currently carries
  the Gemini 1B claim" in another, while the Directory entry it cites
  literally carries that claim.
- The usage-statistics half of the sentence is true, and a full post about
  the announcement (attribution, app-specific scope, statistics, context)
  is genuinely absent — the filing itself is defensible and its "Done when"
  checklist already tells the author to connect to the Directory without
  repeating it. But the record's claim that nothing on the site carries the
  claim is measured false, and the changelog's "none covered on the site"
  is permanent once merged. Per the review contract, a falsified claim in
  the record is blocking on its own.

Requested change (narrow): correct the item's "Nothing on the site currently
carries the Gemini 1B claim" to acknowledge the Directory's milestone line
(and reword the changelog's "none covered on the site" so it does not sweep
the Gemini milestone under it — the statistics and the post treatment remain
uncovered). Everything else in the round verified as claimed.
