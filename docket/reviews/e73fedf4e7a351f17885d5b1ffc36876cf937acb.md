same UTC arithmetic (`setUTCDate(getUTCDate() - ((getUTCDay() + 6) % 7))`):
today (2026-08-15) maps to week 2026-08-10, which holds exactly 8 posts
(frontier-cyber 08-10; claude-code-auto-mode, cyber-eval-cascade,

1`. `mondayOf` in `scripts/dispatch.mjs` (lines 110-114) is character-for-
character identical to `check-publishing-quota.mjs`'s (lines 194-198). The
dispatcher's prediction that a post dated today would breach the week cap
(3 + 1 > 3) is correct. Not falsified.

## Claim 9 — the disclosed CI gap

Read `.github/workflows/loop.yml` (not touched). The "Build prompt" step
passes only `--track` and `--origin` to `scripts/build-prompt.mjs`; the
dispatcher's `reason` output reaches only the "Report the outcome" summary
step. `round.mjs start` on the other hand extracts the full `reason:` line
and passes it as `--reason` (scripts/round.mjs lines 309-328), and
`build-prompt.mjs` renders it ("The dispatcher chose it: ${reason}"). So the
entry's disclosure — a scheduled run's prompt says "forced by hand" whatever
dispatch chose — is accurate. The gap is disclosed without fixing, exactly
as the entry says.
