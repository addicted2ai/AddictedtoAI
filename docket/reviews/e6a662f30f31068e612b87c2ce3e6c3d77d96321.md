## Why this is blocking

The gate's stated purpose, in this round's own words, is to make "the 31st
author item impossible". I made the 31st, 32nd and 33rd author items, with
every check green, on a committed branch. The changelog asserts the field
"cannot quietly become a free-text escape hatch that empties the queue" —
the assertion is true only in the narrowest sense (free *text* is rejected);
as an escape hatch that empties the *counts*, the field works with its single
accepted value, and this very entry teaches every future run the field
exists. A gate that the filing run can walk by copying one documented line is
the round-78 failure shape this round claims to be closing. Everything else
in the round is verified and good; the fix is narrow, and the proofs it
should add are the negative one (a new item carrying `blocked-on: maintainer`
on an over-budget track must go red) and the positive control (the two
settings items still green).
