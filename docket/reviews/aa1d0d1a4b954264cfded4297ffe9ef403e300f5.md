## The rejection

The guard's shape check is `/^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$/` plus
`Number.isNaN(Date.parse(date))` (line 86). `Date.parse` does not reject
every impossible calendar date — it silently rolls some over — so the check
accepts dates that are not real, contradicting its own failure message ("not
a real YYYY-MM-DD date") and the header's "fails loudly if ... a post lacks a
real datePublished". Measured, exit 0 on each, tree reverted after each:

- `datePublished: "2026-02-31"` → `ok 10 posts`, exit 0
  (`Date.parse` yields 2026-03-03).
- `datePublished: "2026-02-29"` (2026 is not a leap year) → `ok 10 posts`,
  exit 0 (`Date.parse` yields 2026-03-01).
- `datePublished: "2026-04-31"` → exit 0 (`Date.parse` yields 2026-05-01).
- Also accepted: 2026-06-31, 2026-09-31, 2026-11-31; rejected (NaN): 2026-01-32,
  2026-03-32, 2026-08-32, 2026-12-32 — the acceptance is calendar-arbitrary.

This is not cosmetic. The site's feed renders `new Date(datePublished)
.toUTCString()` (app/feed.xml/route.js:42), so a post dated "2026-04-31"
publishes on the feed as 2026-05-01. A scratch holding two new posts dated
"2026-05-01" and "2026-04-31" — both of which the feed renders as Friday,
01 May 2026 — exited 0 with `ok 11 posts`: the check bucketed them as two
different days and weeks (mondayOf rolls "2026-04-31" into the week of
2026-04-27), so two posts the site publishes on the same day passed a cap of
1/day. The day cap is bypassable with a calendar-impossible date, and the
week bucket for the rolled date is a week no real post shares.

The changelog's claim "a wrong-shape date → exit 1" is therefore falsified
by measurement: 2026-02-31, 2026-02-29, 2026-04-31, 2026-06-31, 2026-09-31,
2026-11-31 are wrong-shape dates and the check exits 0 on each.

## Exact change required

In `postsFromModule` (scripts/check-publishing-quota.mjs:86), reject any
date that does not round-trip as a real calendar date, e.g. add the
condition that `date === new Date(date + "T00:00:00Z").toISOString().slice(0,
10)` (verified: true for 2026-08-14 and 2026-08-17, false for every date
above). Re-prove the battery with the calendar-impossible dates — each must
exit 1 — and with the two-post "2026-05-01" + "2026-04-31" pair, which must
exit 1 rather than `ok`. Then re-run `node scripts/round.mjs check` green.
