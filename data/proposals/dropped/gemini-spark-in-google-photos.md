# Declined: Gemini Spark in Google Photos

## The story considered

Announced 5 September 2026: subscribed users can ask an agent to search,
edit, curate and share albums and run scheduled photo workflows against
their library, rolling out in the US first for eligible Gemini AI Pro and
Ultra subscribers.

## Which test it failed, and why

**Worth a stranger's attention.** This is a consumer feature rollout,
region-gated and tier-gated. The agentic-photo-library idea is interesting
in the abstract and entirely uncheckable in this one: no artifact, no
measurement, nothing a reader outside the eligible tier can even see. It is
a product announcement, and the site's read surface is not a place product
announcements go to be restated.

## What would make it worth refiling

- A documented failure or privacy consequence of scheduled agentic access to
  a personal photo library, or a published account of what the agent is
  permitted to do without a per-action confirmation. That is a real question
  and this announcement is not the answer to it.
