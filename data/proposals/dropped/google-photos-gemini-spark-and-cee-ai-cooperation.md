# Declined: two stories from 5 September that carried nothing

## The stories considered

Both surfaced in the same 2026-09-06 sweep and both are declined for the
same reason, so they are recorded together rather than padded into two
files.

- **Gemini Spark in Google Photos.** Announced 5 September 2026: subscribed
  users can ask an agent to search, edit, curate and share albums and run
  scheduled photo workflows against their library, rolling out in the US
  first for eligible Gemini AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.
- **Romania joining CEE AI cooperation.** Romania joined eight other Central
  and Eastern European countries — Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, Croatia,
  Hungary, Lithuania, Latvia, Slovenia — in strengthening cooperation on
  artificial intelligence.

## Which test they failed, and why

**Worth a stranger's attention**, for two different reasons that land in the
same place.

*Gemini Spark in Photos* is a consumer feature rollout, region-gated and
tier-gated. The agentic-photo-library idea is interesting in the abstract
and entirely uncheckable in this one: no artifact, no measurement, nothing a
reader outside the eligible tier can even see. It is a product announcement,
and the site's read surface is not a place product announcements go to be
restated.

*The CEE cooperation item* is a diplomatic statement of intent with no
instrument named, no obligation, no date attached to anything happening, and
no way to check later whether it did. The site does cover regulation — it
has published on the EU AI Office's first enforcement RFIs and on a DoJ
statement of interest — and the difference is exactly this: those are
documents with parties and dates, and this is a communiqué.

## What would make them worth refiling

- For Photos: a documented failure or privacy consequence of scheduled
  agentic access to a personal photo library, or a published account of what
  the agent is permitted to do without a per-action confirmation. That is a
  real question and this announcement is not the answer to it.
- For CEE: a named instrument — a joint funding mechanism, a shared
  compute commitment, a coordinated position in an EU AI Act process — with
  a document a reader can open.

`data/proposals/dropped/` is a record, never a block. This slug suppresses
nothing.
