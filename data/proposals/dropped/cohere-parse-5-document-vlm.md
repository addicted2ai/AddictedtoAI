# Declined: Cohere's Parse 5 document-intelligence VLM

## The story considered

The llm-releases feed item (fetched 2026-09-01,
https://llm-releases.com/models/cohere-parse-5): Cohere released Parse 5
(parse-v5.0), a 2.3B document-intelligence vision-language model converting
PDFs, slides and images to structured Markdown with tables, forms and
bounding boxes across nine languages, priced at $1.50 per 1,000 pages via
API, on Model Vault, SageMaker and Azure, positioned on price-to-performance.

## Which test it failed, and why

**Worth a stranger's attention.** The claim that would matter — that a
2.3B VLM beats general-purpose models on document extraction at $1.50 per
thousand pages — rests on vendor benchmarks not fetched in this run, and
the price-to-performance positioning is precisely the kind of claim this
site's readers would want measured rather than repeated. The honest piece
would be an evaluation, not an entry; a scout run cannot produce one, and
an entry repeating the positioning sends to nobody who would forward it.

## What would make it worth refiling

- An independent benchmark or a well-known user's measured comparison of
  Parse 5 against general models on real documents.
- Cohere publishing the evaluation methodology behind the positioning.
- A documented failure mode (misparsed tables, security handling of
  uploaded documents) that gives the story a checkable edge.
