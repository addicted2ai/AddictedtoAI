# Declined: GPT-6 Astra reaching all ChatGPT plans

## The story considered

On 5 September 2026 OpenAI announced that GPT-6 Astra — released 3 September,
pre-trained at Stargate, priced at $10 input / $50 output per million tokens
in the API — would roll out to all ChatGPT plans including Plus over the
following days, with coverage repeating OpenAI's claim of perfect or
near-perfect scores on reasoning benchmarks against GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude
Fable 5.

## Which test it failed, and why

**Worth a stranger's attention.** The site has already published the story
that had something in it: `content/blog/openai-gpt-6-astra-system-card.md`
and `content/blog/openai-astra-critical-designation.md`. What is left is a
tier-availability announcement, which is the most-covered and least
durable thing in AI news — every outlet ran it, nobody will send it, and
the price and availability values belong in the data layer as bound facts
rather than in prose that will rot within the week.

The benchmark claim is not a rescue. It is a vendor's own scoring of its own
model, and this site does not restate a leaderboard position as a fact it
holds on its own authority.

## Weighed against the frontier criteria, and why the flag fails

**F3** — "a release by a covered organisation of a model it positions as its
frontier." The release itself plainly qualified, and the site flagged and
covered it at the time. This is not the release; it is the rollout of an
already-covered release to more subscription tiers, which is a distribution
change, not a frontier event. Flagging it would be flagging the same event
twice because it happened again in a smaller way.

## What would make it worth refiling

- A measured, third-party result that contradicts or materially qualifies
  OpenAI's benchmark claims, published with its method.
- A change in what Astra is permitted to do, rather than who can buy it —
  the restricted cyber capabilities being widened or tightened would be F5
  and would be a real story.
- A withdrawal, a capacity failure, or a documented regression against the
  system card.

`data/proposals/dropped/` is a record, never a block. This slug suppresses
nothing.
