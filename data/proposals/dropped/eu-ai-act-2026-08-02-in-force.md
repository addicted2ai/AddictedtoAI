# Declined: what of the EU AI Act actually took effect on 2026-08-02

## The story considered

The Digital Omnibus on AI, provisionally agreed by Council, Parliament and
Commission negotiators on 2026-05-07, deferred the Chapter III high-risk
obligations: stand-alone Annex III systems to **2027-12-02**, and AI embedded in
Annex I regulated products to **2028-08-02**. The deferral does **not** cover
Article 50, the GPAI enforcement powers, or the penalty regime, all of which
became applicable on **2026-08-02**. A separate strand: providers releasing
models under free and open-source licences with publicly available weights are
excused from two of the four core GPAI obligations (technical documentation and
downstream-provider information) but must still adopt a copyright compliance
policy and publish a training-data summary — and none of that relief reaches
models designated as posing systemic risk.

The candidate piece was "everyone reported the delay; here is the part that
actually bit four weeks ago."

## Which test it failed, and why

**Worth a stranger's attention.** Correct, sourced and forgettable — which this
repository's editorial spec names as a failure rather than a near miss.

The material is true and checkable; that is not the problem. The problem is that
the assembly already exists in volume. Retrieved on 2026-08-31, the first page of
results alone carried client alerts and explainers from Holland & Knight
(2026-04), Gibson Dunn, Travers Smith, Covington's Global Policy Watch and
Inside Global Tech (2026-05-28), two Cloud Security Alliance research notes, the
Linux Foundation Europe explainer, and a dedicated aiacto piece titled "AI Act:
What Really Changes on August 2, 2026". A site with no legal standing adding a
thirteenth summary contributes no assembly, no data and no angle. Nobody sends
it to anybody; they send their own firm's alert.

There is also a timing problem independent of the crowding: 2026-08-02 was four
weeks ago and nothing has happened since to re-open it.

## What would make it worth refiling

Any of the following turns this from a summary into an event with a party and a
date:

- The Commission opens a **first enforcement action or investigation** under the
  GPAI powers that became exercisable on 2026-08-02, against a named provider.
- A named provider is found non-compliant, or publicly changes a model release,
  licence or documentation practice and attributes the change to those powers.
- A provider claims the open-source carve-out for a specific model and the claim
  is contested — the intersection with licence terms is where this site has
  something the law firms do not, because it holds the licence data.
- The deferred Chapter III dates move again.

The last of those is the one to watch alongside
`data/proposals/minimax-h3-excluded-territories.md`: a licence whose Excluded
Territories include the EU raises a real question about what "made available on
the Union market" means, and that question needs a triggering event, not a
recap.
