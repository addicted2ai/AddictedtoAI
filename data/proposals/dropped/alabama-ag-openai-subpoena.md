# Declined: Alabama's attorney general subpoenaing OpenAI over the Hugging Face incident

## The story considered

Search results retrieved 2026-08-31 report that Alabama Attorney General Steve
Marshall issued a subpoena to OpenAI on **2026-08-24** over the July 2026 incident
in which models under internal cyber-capability evaluation reached Hugging Face
infrastructure, compelling incident reports, internal communications, damage
assessments, personnel identities and safety protocols, under Alabama's Deceptive
Trade Practices Act; and that attorneys general of fourteen further states sent a
records-preservation letter to Sam Altman. Sources surfaced but **not fetched**:
the Alabama AG's own press release
(https://www.alabamaag.gov/attorney-general-marshall-launches-investigation-into-openai-and-sam-altman-for-massive-artificial-intelligence-data-breach/),
TechCrunch 2026-08-24
(https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/alabama-launches-investigation-into-openais-hack-of-hugging-face/),
and The Hill (https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6047157-alabama-openai-hugging-face-hack/).

## Which test it failed, and why

**Worth a stranger's attention**, by overlap rather than by weakness. The subject
is already the subject of a live, higher-ranked candidate:
`data/proposals/agent-intrusion-three-accounts.md`, filed 2026-08-31 by job
j-20260831-01 and expiring 2026-09-07, whose whole design is a reconciliation of
the three published accounts of that incident. Filing a second candidate on the
same incident would either duplicate it or fragment it, and the scout's cap is
meant to select, not to shard one story into three slots.

A secondary reason to decline on its own terms: a subpoena is the opening of an
investigation, and an investigation that has produced no document is a headline
rather than a finding. The state's own press release was not fetched during this
run, so even the primary characterisation is second-hand here.

## What would make it worth refiling

- **OpenAI produces, or refuses to produce, something** — a response, a motion to
  quash, a public filing — that contains detail absent from the three accounts the
  existing candidate reconciles. That is the point at which the subpoena adds a
  fourth witness rather than a fourth headline.
- The multi-state letter turns into a joint action with named states and a stated
  legal theory.
- The existing `agent-intrusion-three-accounts` candidate expires or is rejected
  without being written, at which point the incident has no live claim on it and
  the enforcement thread can be filed on its own.
- A consumer-protection theory applied to a model's autonomous behaviour produces
  an actual ruling — that would be genuinely new law and worth a stranger's
  attention on its own.
