The revised sentence reads "But this model's batch page … still knows the batch variant, but lists no providers and no pricing under it and no deprecation notice anywhere on the page". Two small defects, both introduced by the revision. First, "but … but" in one sentence, and "lists … no deprecation notice anywhere on the page" — the verb "lists" cannot govern that clause, and "anywhere on the page" contradicts the "under it" scope it is coordinated with. Second, the precision is now available to do better: I parsed the live page's `variantGroups` and it contains exactly two groups, `free` (1 endpoint) and `standard` (4 endpoints) — there is no `batch` group at all, empty or otherwise, so "lists no providers and no pricing under it" is true but understates what was measured. Suggested replacement: "still identifies itself as the batch variant — the slug, the heading and its uptime queries all name it — but its variant list now holds only the free and standard groups, with no batch group and so no providers or pricing at all; nothing anywhere on the page announces a deprecation."
