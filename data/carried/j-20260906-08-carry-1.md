The body reads "the vendor's own table and the router's endpoint both serve a window four times smaller". Both halves of that ratio are volatile and one of them is already feed-bound: content/wiki/model/aion-labs-aion-rp-llama-3-1-8b.md declares `feeds: openrouter-models: aion-labs/aion-rp-llama-3.1-8b` and a `context_window` fact bound to `context_length` with volatility `fast`. Measured today the ratio is right (model card 131K; OpenRouter `context_length` 32768; the vendor's docs table "Context 32K" on that row), but if the router raises that row the sentence goes quietly wrong with no build signal. Transcluding `{{fact:model/aion-labs-aion-rp-llama-3-1-8b#context_window}}` and letting the reader do the division, or rephrasing to name the window rather than the multiple, removes the rot.
