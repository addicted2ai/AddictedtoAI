The repaired paragraph opens "Nothing about the row stayed flat over that stretch, but what moved was the context window rather than the price." The preceding paragraph spends its whole length proving the opposite — "Same number, five times running" — and the sentence's own second clause concedes it. The opener made sense before the repair, when the thing that moved was the intelligence index; with that sentence gone the claim is left asserting something the page disproves one paragraph earlier. One word fixes it: "Not everything about the row stayed flat over that stretch: what moved was the context window, not the price." Worth noting too that the new opener now states the conclusion the paragraph already ends on ("Price and context window are two separate dials ... they don't turn together"), so the closing line has become a restatement.
