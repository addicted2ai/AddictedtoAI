The gateway paragraph says "The feed does carry both gated models — as release announcements, on 3 and 4 September". Those are the dates the site's feed recorded the rows (changes.jsonl dates 2026-09-03 and 2026-09-04); the announcements' own pubDate is 1 and 2 September, and the post's whole first half establishes 1, 2 and 3 September as the release dates. A reader can take the two date pairs as a contradiction. The neighbouring clause about the generally available twins avoids this by saying they "turned up in the gateway", which reads as observation. Inserting one word — "recorded on 3 and 4 September" — fixes it.
