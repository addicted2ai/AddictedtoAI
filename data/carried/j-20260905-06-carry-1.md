The title says "Three of its nineteen pages" and the first sentence says "the United States filed twenty pages". Both are true of different paginations and I verified both: the ECF stamp reads "Page 20 of 20" on the last page, while the brief's own page numbers run 1-19 (PDF page 20 carries the internal number 19), and the Kadrey attack sits on internal pages 15, 16 and 17 — three pages, exactly as the title says. But the reader meets the contradiction in the first two lines and never gets the explanation. Fix by making the denominator explicit once, e.g. "three of its nineteen numbered pages", or by having the lede say "twenty pages, nineteen of them numbered".
