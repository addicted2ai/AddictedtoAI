The new clause reads "a real lifecycle event: the row's first status flip, so it cannot be a metadata correction of a dropped date." Measured, the openrouter-models change history in data/changes.jsonl spans three days only — 2026-08-29 (29 lines), 2026-08-31 (1), 2026-09-01 (38) — while the row's own `created` timestamp (1780937140, ~June 2026) predates that window by months. So "the row's first status flip" is true of the recorded diff history, not of the row's life, and the absolute "cannot" is one word stronger than the evidence. Inserting "recorded" — "the row's first recorded status flip" — fixes it without touching the reasoning.
