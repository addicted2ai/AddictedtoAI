The repaired sentence now states 26 August twice for the same repo under two different labels — "bartowski's GGUF of Thomson-1.0-Small, latest update 26 August" and, in the parenthetical, "the conversion was created 26 August". Both are true (the API gives createdAt 2026-08-26T04:45:39Z and lastModified 2026-08-26T07:26:55Z, under three hours apart), but a reader meeting the same date twice in one sentence reads it as an editing slip rather than as two distinct facts that happen to coincide. The sentence also now carries an em-dash pair inside a parenthesis inside an em-dash clause. Folding the opening clause to "bartowski's GGUF of Thomson-1.0-Small, created and last updated 26 August" states both facts once and lets the parenthetical shrink to the source repo's date.
