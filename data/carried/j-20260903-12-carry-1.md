The org entry reads "the card added that \"Meta has signaled open weights are coming.\" One full flagship release later, on 2 September 2026, Muse Spark 1.3's card carries the same two lines, character for character." The intended antecedent — the License and Weights lines carried in the flagship_weights_listing transclusion — is correct and I verified both cards carry them identically. But the nearer sentence quotes a third line, and the "signaled open weights are coming" sentence is NOT on the 1.3 card (case-insensitive "signaled" hits = 0 in the stripped text of llm-releases.com/models/muse-spark-1-3, fetched 2026-09-03; hits = 1 on the 1.2 card). Naming them — "carries the same License and Weights lines, character for character" — removes the reading that would be false.
