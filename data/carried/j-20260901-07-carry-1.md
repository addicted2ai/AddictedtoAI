The proposal's evidence line carries "CyberGym 84.5 vs GLM-5.2 77.2; ExploitGym 105/130 vs 29/39". Fetching https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3 on 2026-09-01 confirmed the GLM-5.3 side — license "glm-5.3", 753B params, the "Emergent Cyber Capability" note, CyberGym 84.5, ExploitGym 105/130 — but the extraction surfaced a second ExploitGym row (130/247 at a 6-hour timeout) and did not surface either comparator (77.2, 29/39). The "29/39" denominator matches neither 130 nor 247, so the pair may be reading across two different timeout rows. WebFetch's extractor is not evidence of absence, so this is not a finding that the numbers are wrong — it is that the post job must read the benchmark table itself, state which timeout row each figure comes from, and name the card as the cited document, rather than inheriting the pair from this proposal.
