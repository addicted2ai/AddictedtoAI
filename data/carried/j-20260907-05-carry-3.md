The docket quotes the announcement as "Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS) improvement of up to 60% against IMERG, 30% for MRMS, and 10% against rain gauge measurements". I fetched https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/ on 2026-09-07 and the same sentence ends "...and 10% against rain gauge measurements for early lead times". The docket's own done-when line already requires every accuracy figure to name whoever measured it and against what; the lead-time condition belongs in that list, because a CRPS gain at early lead times is not a medium-range claim. Fix: carry the four words.
