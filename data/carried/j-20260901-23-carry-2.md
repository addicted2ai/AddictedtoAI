The new evidence text calls the two values "the 2-hour timeout budget" and "the 6-hour", which reads as wall-clock. The card's footnote says the budgets are "calculated as the API inference time rescaled by per-model tokens per second rate (per-model TPS sourced from Artificial Analysis; that is, we rescale GLM-5.3's results by 115 TPS, Kimi K3's results by 40 TPS and Qwen3.8 Max's results by 47 TPS), plus the non-API overhead." A 115-vs-40 TPS rescaling is exactly the methodology point a skeptical reader raises about a cross-model exploitation comparison. Fix: add the rescaling to the evidence line's footnote parenthetical so the post inherits it rather than having to rediscover it.
