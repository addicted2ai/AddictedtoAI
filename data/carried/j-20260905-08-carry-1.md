The post writes "Against that, the paper's own general pipeline scored 502.0 on IOI 2025. Hengxi Liu scored 591.23 on the same problems." The paper is more specific: "Our general Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC reaches 304 without code-specific RL and 502.0 after five rounds of GenCorrect", and Figure 8a gives the round-by-round climb from 343.9 to 502.0. A reader comparing 502.0 against a human's contest score cannot tell from the post that the 502.0 side had five rounds and 200 generations per round behind it. "General pipeline" gestures at this but does not say it. Adding four words — "after five rounds of GenCorrect" — would fix it, and the job's own done-when line asked for exactly this distinction ("the GenCorrect contribution distinguished from post-training"). Not blocking: the sentence is used against the model, not for it, so the omission does not flatter the claim.
