The blockquote rendered as OpenAI's words opens "We and the larger AI community do not yet have a clear standard...". I fetched the declared anchor and searched its bytes: "We and the larger AI community do not yet have a clear standard" is MISSING; what the page carries is TechCrunch's own bridge — OpenAI's statement "stating that both OpenAI and 'the larger AI community do not yet have a clear standard for how to report misalignment that shows up during training, evaluation, and deployment, including examples that don't look like traditional security incidents but could provide insight into AI behavior and future risks.'" Everything from "the larger" onward is verbatim; the two words "We and" are the post's own reconstruction of a pronoun TechCrunch elided. The meaning is identical and the primary (the X post) is unreachable, so this is not a wrong claim — but the post's own sourcing block says "Every OpenAI quotation above is TechCrunch's rendering of that post", and for two words it is not. Fix: open the blockquote at "the larger AI community" and lead in with TechCrunch's own framing ("OpenAI's statement said that both it and...").
