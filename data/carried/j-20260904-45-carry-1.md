Neither mention is hyperlinked, and the only 3.8-era link anywhere near them is [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/02/gemini-3-8-flash-launch/) at line 68. Both quotes are verbatim in https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/ (verified from raw bytes for this review), so nothing here is wrong — a reader just cannot get from the quote to the document. Fix: link that URL on "the launch post" at line 93, as `content/wiki/model/google-gemini-3-8-flash.md` already does for the same launch.
