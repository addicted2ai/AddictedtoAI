The brief states the `feeds` map "is the join the board relies on". It is not. `lib/render/frontier.mjs:50` `matchProviders()` matches an org to catalog providers by normalised alias substring — its own comment says "matched to the catalog providers it stands behind by alias, never invented" — and the module never reads `feeds` at all. No org entry in the corpus carries a `feeds` map (grep over content/wiki/org: no matches), and there is no source whose row ids an org could bind to anyway — the two registered sources are openrouter-models (rows are models) and llm-releases. Repeating the sentence pushes future entry jobs toward declaring a binding that would be wrong.
