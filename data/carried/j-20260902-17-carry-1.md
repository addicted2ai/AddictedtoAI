The proposal now carries the card's qualifier verbatim, but the card explains it nowhere — `grep -n -i "fallback"` over the fetched card returns exactly one line, the table header at line 22, and no footnote, caption or prose says what fallback was enabled, to what, or for which runs. That is a trap for the writer: a qualifier reproduced without a definition invites a plausible gloss ("with fallback routing", "with a fallback model"), which would be an invention attributed to Z.ai. One clause in the evidence block — that the qualifier appears only in the header and the card states no meaning for it — closes it, and is also the strongest version of the like-for-like point: the one column the card qualifies is the one column it declines to explain.
