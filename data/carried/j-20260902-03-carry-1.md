The revised summary says GLM-5.3 stands "third on the card's own row (behind Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol)" — literally accurate, but the card's ExploitGym footnote reads "We evaluate GLM-5.3, Kimi-K3 and Qwen3.8 Max in Claude Code 2.1.207" and names TPS rescaling factors for exactly those three (115 / 40 / 47). So the ranking the summary now cedes is a comparison between a number Z.ai measured under a stated method and three numbers whose method the card does not state.
