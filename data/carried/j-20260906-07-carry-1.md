`lib/declined-fields.test.mjs` enforces the shrink-only claim with `assert.ok(green.known <= DEBT_CEILING_2026_09_06)` against a literal `const DEBT_CEILING_2026_09_06 = 48`. That binds the debt to its 2026-09-06 high-water mark, not to its current value: once `data/declined-binding-debt.json` is paid down — the filed proposal's done-when takes it to zero — the constant still permits up to 48 forgiven bindings, so the list could regrow to 48 with the suite green. The file's note says "This list has one legal direction, down", and today the mechanism enforces only "never above 48". Nothing is wrong as shipped (`known` is 48 and the ceiling is 48, so it is currently exact); the fix is a discipline the artifacts do not yet state — whoever retires debt lowers the constant to the new count in the same diff. One sentence in `data/declined-binding-debt.json`'s `note`, beside "This list has one legal direction, down", would make it a rule rather than a hope.
