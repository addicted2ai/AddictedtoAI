`lib/units.mjs` declares `openrouter-models|pricing.prompt` and `pricing.completion` as `USD per token`, so `displayQuantity` scales them and names the scale — I ran it: `{"value":"$5.00","unit":"per million tokens"}` and `{"value":"$25.00","unit":"per million tokens"}`, and `.fact-unit` is visible in `app/globals.css:1102`. The rendered sentence therefore says "per million tokens" three times in about twenty words: "$30.00 in / $150.00 out per million tokens against the standard row's $5.00 per million tokens / $25.00 per million tokens". The sibling page `content/wiki/model/anthropic-claude-opus-4-7.md` avoids this by never stating the unit in prose at all. Fix is one clause — "…$30.00 in / $150.00 out, against the standard row's {{fact…}} / {{fact…}}" — letting the bound values carry the scale for both sides. Cosmetic, render-only, no claim moves.
