Every `lib/render/frontier.mjs` line citation in the new "What an `org` entry declares" section was computed against the file as it stood BEFORE this same diff inserted its 29-line block comment at line 50, so each is short by exactly 29 in the merged tree. Measured 2026-09-06 in this worktree at `4ebc33db7002`: `matchProviders` is at 79 (cited `:50-61`), `orgOwnDomains` at 346 (cited `:317-326`, with "the test at `:323`" now at 352), `isVendorSourced` at 358 (cited `:329-341`), `claimRank` at 373 (cited `:344-352`, and "`:340`" is now 369), the registrable-domain comment carrying the `blog.google` / `deepmind.google` example at 337-344 (cited `:309-315`), and the `renderPlayersBoard` no-filter comment at 567 (cited `:535-549`). The citations to `pulse/lib/corpus.mjs`, `pulse/lib/mint.mjs`, `lib/changes.mjs` and `lib/schema.mjs` are unaffected and all resolve correctly, and the block comment inside `frontier.mjs` itself cites no line numbers in its own file.
