The candidate's summary says "the vulnerability class Manifold Security disclosed on 2026-09-02" and its why-now says the agents were "still unpatched at disclosure three days ago". Fetching https://www.manifold.security/blog/ai-coding-agents-git-hijack on 2026-09-05 returns a byline date of "Sep 1, 2026", and an independent search corroborates it — "On Monday, Sep 1, 2026, Manifold Security published a research post by Francisco Rosales that names a class called GitSpawn", with "four of the eight flaws remained unpatched as of September 1". 2026-09-02 is The Hacker News's publication date, not the disclosure date — the docket's own evidence block correctly dates the THN piece 2026-09-02, so the two look to have been merged. Fix: the authoring job should read the Manifold page's raw HTML, take the disclosure date from it, and keep the THN date attached to THN. The why-now's "still unpatched at disclosure three days ago" needs the same correction.
