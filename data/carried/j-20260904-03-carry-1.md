The post says "more than 35 partner products and partner-operated services across the Daybreak Defense Network, per the announcement." Every independent retrieval of the announcement renders the phrase as "more than 35 ENTERPRISE products and partner-operated services" — the products are enterprise security tools reached through partners, which is not the same claim as 35 products belonging to partners. Since the sentence explicitly attributes to the announcement, change "partner products" to "enterprise products".
