The record's cohere-command-a.md:72 line quotes "with a 256000 tokens context window" and reasons that "the transclusion fills the adjective slot of the noun phrase 'context window'"; meta-muse-glimmer-30b.md:98 quotes "over a 131072 tokens context window" with "same reading as above". Neither page is written that way. cohere-command-a.md:71-72 reads "listed {{fact:...#listed_date}} with a context window of\n{{fact:... #context_window}} — a", and meta-muse-glimmer-30b.md:97-98 reads "output over\na context window of {{fact:...#context_window}}. The" — in both the transclusion FOLLOWS the noun phrase, so the pages render "with a context window of 256000 tokens — a" and "over a context window of 131072 tokens.". The verdict (not a doubling) is unaffected — nothing literal follows the unit in either — but the quoted rendering and the "adjective slot" reasoning describe a construction that is not on the page. Fixing it means re-quoting both lines in the page's own word order and replacing the adjective-slot reason with the one that actually applies (the unit is followed by punctuation, not by a literal).
