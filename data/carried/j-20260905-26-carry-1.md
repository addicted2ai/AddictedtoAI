The body says m2-her "carries a window of {{fact:model/minimax-minimax-m2-her#context_window}} where every other M2-series row carries {{fact:model/minimax-minimax-m2#context_window}}". Measured against data/sources/openrouter-models/latest.json (fetched_at 2026-09-05T06:00:04.599Z, row_count 431), that universal is false for one row: minimax/minimax-m2.7:free carries context_length 196608, not 204800. m2, m2.1, m2.5 and m2.7 all carry 204800; all six entries bind context_window to feed openrouter-models path context_length, so those are the rendered values. The entry counts rows strictly elsewhere ("Ten of the eleven MiniMax rows"), so the row-level reading is the entry's own. The contrast survives intact — 65,536 against ~200,000 either way — but the quantifier does not. "every other main-line M2 row", or naming the free variant's smaller window, would fix it.
