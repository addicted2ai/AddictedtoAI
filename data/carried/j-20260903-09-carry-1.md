The gemini-3-8-flash docket attributes two things to https://9to5google.com/2026/09/02/gemini-3-8-flash-launch/ that I could not reproduce from that page with my own extractor: Wiz's "+7.5-9.7% recall" (the page as I retrieved it says only "higher recall on their internal penetration testing benchmark for a 2.3-5.2x lower cost", with no percentage range), and the sentence "At times, the model might use more tokens to maximize performance, especially at higher effort levels" (I retrieved the adjacent "works harder" framing as "exhibit[s] greater diligence — executing extra reasoning steps, and calling tools iteratively"). This is NOT a finding that either is wrong — WebFetch's extractor denies text that is present as readily as it invents text, so absence through it proves nothing, and every other figure in this docket reproduced exactly. It is a flag that these two are the only unconfirmed items in the set: the entry job that picks this candidate up should quote whichever sentence the page actually carries, and drop the percentage range if it is not there rather than carry it forward on the docket's authority.
