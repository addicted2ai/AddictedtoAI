"Who it lands on" opens "If you or an affiliate runs hosted inference on GLM-5.3 and your group's revenue ... is $10B or more". Clause 2's first condition is "If the Licensee or any of its affiliates operates a Model as a Service business" — with no tie to the Software anywhere in the sentence. Read literally, a $10B group that operates a MaaS business on some other model, and merely fine-tunes GLM-5.3 internally, is inside the condition. The post's narrower reading is the sensible commercial one, but a piece whose subject is what the clause literally binds should say the text does not say it.
