The brief required `publishes_from`; it cannot be declared today. `entrySchema` in `lib/schema.mjs` is `.strict()` and rejects the key — measured: `entrySchema.safeParse({...entry, publishes_from: ['bytedance.com']})` returns `Unrecognized key: "publishes_from"` — and `grep publishes_from lib/` returns no matches, because the field lives only in the pending `separate-a-claim-from-a-fact` amendment. The live substitute already covers part of the ground: `orgNameTokens` in `lib/render/frontier.mjs` derives the token `bytedance` from this entry's display name, so a claim cited from `seed.bytedance.com` attributes correctly with no declaration. What it does not cover is the domain this entry itself establishes as the only place the model can be called from abroad — `ai.byteplus.com`, whose registrable label `byteplus` is not one of the org's name tokens. When the field lands, this entry needs `publishes_from: [byteplus.com]`, or a real BytePlus-hosted Seed claim renders as the honest-looking blank FM-N6 describes.
