The post writes "Its benchmark table averages 74.6 across the board, against 71.2 for Gemma 4-31B and 68.2 for Haiku 4.5" and cites only the two competitor columns. The same table I fetched from the model card also carries Snowdon-1.1-Small at 71.7 and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 71.7 — so the $40M bought +2.9 points over the free Apache-2.0 base. Sharper still, on the row the post does quote, Thomson's Stanford LegalBench 79.9 is BELOW its own base (Qwen 80.3, Snowdon 80.9), and on MBE Bar Exam 83.4 barely clears Snowdon's 83.1. Nothing in the post is false — it never claims Thomson beats its base — but the base columns are the strongest available evidence against the parity story and they are left on the table. A future post or wiki fact on Thomson-1.0-Small should carry them.
