`proposal.md` says "Resolved as keeper ruling K46" and `specs/blog/spec.md` says "The bar was withdrawn as keeper ruling K46". The record does not support "keeper". `loops/ui-loop/state.md:59` records it as "**K46** (BLIND-002)" with no keeper attribution, in a list that marks keeper decisions explicitly ("K23 keeper, K24–K29 delegated"; "**K43** (keeper, 2026-09-05)"; "**K45** (keeper via orchestrator)"). BLIND-002's own "Scope of the ruling" says the opposite in as many words: "This is not escalated: under K40 it is far short of 'insane catastrophic project threatening'." K46 was taken under K40 blanket delegation while the keeper was away. Replacing "keeper ruling K46" with "ruling K46, taken under the K40 delegation" in both places fixes it, and it matters precisely because the passage exists to tell a later reader who decided this and on what authority.
