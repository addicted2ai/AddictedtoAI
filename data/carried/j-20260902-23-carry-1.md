The rewritten sentence keeps "your group's revenue over any trailing 12 months is $10B or more". The fetched LICENSE says "exceeds 10 billion US dollars (or the equivalent in other currencies) in total over any consecutive 12 months". Two slips in one clause: "or more" includes a group at exactly $10.0B, which "exceeds" does not, and "trailing" narrows the window to one ending now, where "any consecutive" reaches any 12-month span. The post's own analysis section gets the window right ("over any consecutive 12 months, so a fiscal year is the wrong window to check against"), and the title and lede get the threshold right ("above $10B", "passes $10B"), so this is the one place the closing summary drifts from the quoted text. "over any consecutive 12 months is above $10B" fixes both.
