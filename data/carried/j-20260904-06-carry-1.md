The revision did not touch this, and pass 1 raised it as a carry rather than a blocker, so it survives into the merged entry. `blue_definition` is OpenAI's verbatim sentence "Daybreak Blue supports common defensive work with our mainline models". I read `renderFact` in `lib/facts.mjs` (the `cited` branch, lines 249-257): the value is emitted bare inside `<span class="fact-value">`, escaped, with no quotation marks, followed by "source, accessed 2026-09-04". Inlined mid-sentence in the body's second paragraph, the built page therefore says "with our mainline models" in this site's voice, about models this site does not own. `red_definition` has no such problem because OpenAI wrote that one in the third person. Changing the value to "with OpenAI's mainline models" keeps it faithful and stops the site claiming the models.
