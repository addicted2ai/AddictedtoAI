Lines 51-55 read "the standard row's price, whose prompt rate fell from $0.625 to $0.60 and whose completion rate fell from $3.125 to $2.40 per million tokens", dated "as observed on 3 September 2026". That is true of the 2026-09-03 snapshot and is not a defect. But the move has since reversed: I read every historical blob of data/sources/openrouter-models/{latest,previous}.json out of git and the standard row runs $0.50/$2.20 through 2026-09-01, $0.625/$3.125 on 2026-09-02, $0.60/$2.40 on 2026-09-03, and $0.625/$3.125 again on 2026-09-04; data/derived/feed-rows.json agrees, carrying prompt "0.000000625" and completion "0.000003125" at $as_of 2026-09-04. A reader arriving today therefore reads a price cut that no longer holds, while the sibling entry content/wiki/model/nvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b.md renders the restored $0.625/$3.125 live — two pages of this corpus disagreeing about the same row's price with nothing on either to reconcile them. The author knows: the HTML comment added by this job says "the to-values no longer match the live row (the 2026-09-04 snapshot lists $0.625 / $3.125 again)". The fix is to move that fact out of the comment and into the prose — a clause noting the 2026-09-04 restoration, or a transclusion of the live values beside the dated move — so the reader sees what the comment already tells the next author.
