The post reads "...smaller bursts on 1 and 2 July. Leitner went on deleting agent pages for five weeks after that." WinBuzzer, the cited source, says he "continued removing agent-created pages for five weeks after the main burst ended" — and the main burst ended after 22 June, not after 2 July. As written, "after that" attaches the five weeks to the July bursts and moves the end of the cleanup about ten days later than the source puts it. Fix: "for five weeks after the main burst ended."
