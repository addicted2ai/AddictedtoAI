The repaired sentence is 'The threshold, in OpenAI's words, means that "with the right tools and access, it can find previously unknown security flaws and develop ways to exploit them across many well-protected systems without a person guiding each step"'. On the announcement page the "it" is Astra, because the page's clause hangs off "We now believe Astra meets the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold ... meaning that with the right tools and access, it can find ...". In the post the nearest grammatical antecedent is "The threshold", which is not what OpenAI said and not what the post means. No factual claim moves and the quote is exact, so this is cosmetic — but it was clearer before the repair, which read "means Astra can". Fix: bracket the subject inside the quote — '"with the right tools and access, [Astra] can find ..."' — or recast the frame to "means that Astra, with the right tools and access, can find ...", whichever keeps the quoted span intact.
