The proposal says of `MATERIAL_KINDS`: "`price`, `context` and `status` are material **field** names — they travel on a line's `field`". Parsing all 182 lines, the `field` values actually carried are `price_input` (8), `price_output` (8), `status` (6) and `context_window` (1). Only `status` travels verbatim; `price` and `context` are the spec's and registry's names for the material-field *categories* (`data/sources/registry.json` declares `price_input`, `price_output`, `context_window`). The load-bearing half of the sentence is exactly right and I confirmed it — all three appear as a `kind` on zero of the 182 lines — so this is a dropped qualifier, not a wrong claim. "are the material field categories, carried on a line's `field` as `price_input`, `price_output` and `context_window`" would fix it.
