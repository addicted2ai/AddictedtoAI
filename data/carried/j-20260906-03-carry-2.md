`portal_catalog` states "332 distinct named model rows, counted from the landing page's own per-1M price cells". Fetching https://portal.nousresearch.com/ on 2026-09-06 (the fact's own `accessed` date) and extracting the embedded `{"displayName","modelName","tokenPrice"}` rows gives 346 rows, 346 distinct `modelName`, 337 distinct `displayName`, and 333 distinct `modelName` once the 13 `~`-prefixed free-tier duplicate ids are dropped. No counting convention I tried lands on 332; the nearest is 333. Same-day drift of one row is the likely explanation and this is not blocking, but the figure should either be re-counted with the convention stated ("distinct model ids, free-tier duplicates excluded") or softened. The load-bearing half of the claim is exactly right and I confirmed it: of all 346 rows, zero match /nous|hermes|nomos|consilience|psyche/ in either id or display name.
