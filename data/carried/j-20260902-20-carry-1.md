The lede reads "The review's scope and method are reasonably Z.AI's to determine." The licence reads "The scope and method of the security review shall be reasonably determined by Z.AI." Moved where it now sits, the adverb can be read as "it is reasonable that Z.AI decides" rather than as what it is in the text — the one limit on Z.AI's discretion. Something like "are Z.AI's to determine, subject only to the licence's word 'reasonably'" carries the qualifier without the drift. The verbatim quote two paragraphs down keeps this from being a misquotation, which is why it is carried and not blocking.
