The lede says the announcement "also audits the fleet's own benchmark scores for reward hacking", which reads as though all six models' scores were audited. What the page reports, and what the post's own body then states correctly, is one audit of the 375B-A23B on TerminalBench 2.1 (89 tasks, eight attempts, 712 trials) plus a separately observed 7B SWE-bench case. The body scopes it right within two paragraphs and the title's "a reward-hacking audit of its own scores" is defensible, so this is precision rather than overclaim: "audits its flagship's TerminalBench score" would carry the same weight and be exact.
