The post says "The rest of the file is clause 1 (the MIT-style grant, the notice condition, and a requirement that use comply with applicable laws)". In the LICENSE fetched 2026-09-02 the grant is the unnumbered paragraph above the numbered list — it opens "Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person or entity (the Licensee) obtaining a copy of this software" and closes "subject to the following conditions:" — and clause 1 is exactly two sentences, the notice condition and the applicable-laws sentence, with no grant language in it. The post's argument in "The condition attaches to all commercial use, not just MaaS" leans on the grant being made subject to the conditions, which is a preamble fact, so naming it correctly strengthens that section.
