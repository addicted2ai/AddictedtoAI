The parenthetical reads "(the source repo is recent enough, created 18 August, that its window spans its whole life)". Singling out the source repo implies the GGUF's rolling window truncates its count. It does not: the Hugging Face API gives bartowski's conversion `createdAt: 2026-08-26T04:45:39.000Z` (fetched 2026-09-03), eight days before publication, so its 30-day window also covers its whole life and both figures are in fact lifetime totals. A fix that keeps the caveat honest: "both repos are recent enough — the conversion was created 26 August, the source repo 18 August — that each window spans its whole life".
