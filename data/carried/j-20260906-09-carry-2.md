`lib/render/entry.mjs:87` renders every fact in a standalone Facts section, independent of the body. Read there, some values are missing their subject because they were written to be dropped into a transcluding sentence: `only_dead_upstream_in_the_catalog` begins "the only one. 179 of the 431 rows…", `mai_rows_on_this_router` begins "none. Across all 431 rows…", and `wizardlm_router_description` ends with the editorial aside "— OpenRouter's own catalog copy, not Microsoft's". Rewriting these three values to stand alone (and moving the aside into the body, which already makes the same point) would leave the prose reading identically while making the Facts section legible on its own.
