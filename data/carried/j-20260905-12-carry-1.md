The post now writes "The first round scored 343.9" and (pre-existing) "scored 502.0 on IOI 2025". Both numbers are means. The paper reads "As shown in Figure 8(a), Nano-CC's mean IOI 2025 score increases from 360.6 after the first round to 468.2 after five rounds ... Ultra-CC improves from 343.9 to 502.0", and Figure 8(a)'s caption is "Mean IOI 2025 score across successive GenCorrect rounds for Nano-CC and Ultra-CC ... shaded regions show the min-max range from 5 runs per round" (arXiv:2609.02849v1, HTML and PDF, retrieved 5 September 2026). The post is scrupulous about this two screens earlier — "That mean is 521.72, with an observed range of 495.0 to 545.8" — so the inconsistency is local. "averaged 343.9", or "the mean first round scored 343.9", fixes it. Not blocking: the paragraph argues against the model, and a mean is the fairer of the two readings anyway.
