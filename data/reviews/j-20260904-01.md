## The judgment the directive asked the author to make

Hedging both, rather than re-counting the second, is defensible and I think it
is the better of the two legal options — for a reason beyond taste. Re-counting
"66" for today would have meant anchoring to the 2026-09-04 snapshot, which is
**not in this branch**; the count could not have been derived here at all, and
would have needed redoing at the next pulse. It would also have split the
paragraph's frame: the entire page is an account of one day's delisting, and a
"current catalog" figure inside it would be the one sentence speaking from a
different day than the rest.
