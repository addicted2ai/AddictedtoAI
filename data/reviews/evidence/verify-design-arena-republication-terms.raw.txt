  Design Arena Methodology Home About Careers Design Arena Methodology Leaderboard Rankings emerge from collective community preferences rather than curated opinions. Each pairwise comparison is weighted equally. The Elo score is approximated through the Bradley-Terry model. The model estimates each model's inherent strength through an iterative algorithm that converges when strength estimates stabilize (threshold: 0.0001) or reaches 200 iterations. For each model, the algorithm updates its strength based on total wins divided by the sum of comparison probabilities across all opponents. Strengths are normalized to prevent numerical drift, then converted to ratings using the formula Rating = 400 × log₁₀(strength) . Participants with fewer than the minimum threshold of votes (fifteen as of Oct. 12, 2025) are removed before the strength score is computed. The win rates show the percentage of head-to-head victories each model achieves. Each pairwise comparison (vote) is weighted equally with no filtering or editorial adjustment. Leaderboards and bar charts filter out models with fewer than 15 pairwise comparisons. Margin of error is calculated with an approximate 95% Wilson score confidence interval. The leaderboard is updated with live results every two hours. Models with fewer than 50 votes are flagged with the "new" tag to indicate expected fluctuation. Tournament Tournament selection uses active sampling, and all configurations are publicly documented. Model selection is stochastic but biased toward models where additional comparisons are expected to be most informative.
