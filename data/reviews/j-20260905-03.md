## The one thing that could have made this an event by accident

The diff adds three `benchmarks.artificial_analysis.*` paths to the source's
`yields` array. If `yields` drove anything, a change whose stated decision is
"not an event" would have created 165 changed-feed lines on the next Pulse run —
the precise opposite of what it claims. It does not: `yields` is read nowhere in
`pulse/`, `lib/`, `loop/` or `scripts/` except one shape assertion in
`pulse/tests/registry.test.mjs:35` (`Array.isArray(s.yields) && s.yields.length > 0`).
Every other `yields` hit in a `.mjs` file is the English word in a comment
(`pulse/lib/queue.mjs:593`, `pulse/lib/corpus.mjs:146`,
`pulse/lib/corroboration.mjs:61`). What actually builds a catalog column, an
entry fact and a changed-feed line is `material_fields`, read by
`pulse/lib/diff.mjs`, `pulse/lib/derive.mjs` and `pulse/lib/mint.mjs`; the diff
adds nothing there, and the new guard would now throw if someone tried to add it
there without removing the refusal. So `yields` here is inventory — "the source
serves this" — which is exactly the role `specs/pulse` gives it ("what fields it
yields"), and the decision holds.

## Spec and scope

`specs/pulse`, "Sources live in a registry and refusals are data", says adding
or removing a source is an ordinary data change, not an OpenSpec change; it
enumerates what the registry SHALL record without forbidding more. A new
optional per-source key with its own loader validation is inside that. Nothing
in `openspec/specs/` is touched, nor `data/config.json`, `runners.yml` or
`STOP`. The diff is four files: the brief and `source.json` the loop wrote, the
registry entry, the loader, and its tests. No content file is touched. That is
the stated outcome and not a line more.

`addictedtoai-eexr`, cited in both the code comment and the registry note as
carrying the un-done part, exists and is open: "openrouter-models: 23 more
source paths are in the undecided state declined_fields just ended for
benchmarks", and its body enumerates all 23 with row counts. The note's "23
further paths this source serves are still in it" is therefore not a round
number invented to sound measured. Deferring the general check to its own issue
rather than widening this job is the right call and the right mechanism.
