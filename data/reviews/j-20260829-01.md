## Why this is a revise

Two of the five are recorded as clean matches when the source is qualified and
the entry is not. The evidence file quotes the source honestly in its own
table — nothing is concealed — but it then fills in `verdict: match` for both
and closes with "All five facts hold as written." That last line is the claim
under review, and on two of five it is written from the sense of the sentence
rather than from a strict comparison of entry text against source text.

**1. `former_name` — the source qualifies it and the same page arguably
contradicts the unqualified form.**

Entry: `"Zhipu AI, until the 2025 rebranding"`. Source (idx=3726):

```
The company was formerly known as Zhipu AI outside China until its
rebranding in 2025.
```

"outside China" is load-bearing and the entry drops it. The same page's infobox
(idx=1909) is the reason it matters:

```
Trade name Z.ai Zhipu AI (China) Native name 北京智谱华章科技有限公司
```

Zhipu AI is listed there as a *current* China-facing trade name, not a retired
one. The evidence file's "Out of scope" paragraph goes further and asserts that
the `former_name` field "concerns the public brand name and remains correct" —
that specific assertion is the one the infobox undercuts, since the public
brand name in China is still Zhipu AI. The brief asked for exactly this to be
surfaced: "report anything that no longer matches rather than quietly
correcting it." It was neither reported nor corrected.

**2. `listing` — the source hedges, the entry asserts.**

Entry: `"Hong Kong Stock Exchange, 8 January 2026 — the first major Chinese LLM
company to IPO"`. Source (idx=7292 and idx=4421):

```
On 8 January 2026, Z.ai held its IPO on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange to
become a listed company.
```
```
It is considered to be China's first major LLM company that went through an
initial public offering , on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in January 2026,
closing the day at US$558 million .
```

The date and the exchange match exactly. The superlative does not: the source
says "It is considered to be", the entry states it as fact. This fact's
volatility is `dated`, meaning it is never re-checked, so a stripped hedge here
is permanent.

### Required changes

1. Record `former_name` as a qualified match, not `match`, naming the dropped
   "outside China" and the infobox's `Trade name … Zhipu AI (China)` row; and
   withdraw or correct the "remains correct" assertion in the "Out of scope"
   paragraph.
2. Record `listing` as a qualified match, naming the source's "is considered to
   be" against the entry's flat superlative.
3. Replace "All five facts hold as written" with what was actually observed —
   three clean, two carrying a source qualifier the entry drops — and flag both
   for the maintainer as a reported mismatch rather than editing
   `content/wiki/org/z-ai.md`, per the brief.
