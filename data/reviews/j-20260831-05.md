## Verdict

`revise`. One defect, and it is narrow: a quotation rendered inside quotation
marks that METR did not write. Everything else in an unusually heavily-sourced
piece checked out verbatim against the primaries.

## The defect

The post writes (line 100–103):

> What they did not examine is on the record too: "All other questions were out
> of scope for this investigation, including the effectiveness of safeguards, the
> extent of the security compromise that occurred, and the effectiveness of
> OpenAI's investigation process."

Fetched `https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/`
on 2026-08-31 and asked for the complete sentence containing "the effectiveness
of safeguards", verbatim. What METR actually wrote:

> "We agreed all other questions were also out of scope, including the
> effectiveness of safeguards, the extent of the security compromise that
> occurred, and the effectiveness of OpenAI's investigation process and planned
> remediation steps."

Asked separately and explicitly whether the exact string "All other questions
were out of scope for this investigation" appears on the page, the answer was
that it does not.

Two problems, and the first is the one that matters:

1. **"We agreed" is gone.** METR's sentence says the scope was *agreed* — a
   boundary METR and OpenAI drew together. The post's version reads as a
   condition imposed on METR's investigation. In a piece whose entire thesis is
   that each account's boundary was drawn by someone and you should know by whom
   — the post makes exactly this point two paragraphs earlier, "METR and Redwood
   were handed a wider frame and say who drew it" — this is the wrong two words
   to lose. It is a small edit that moves the sentence in the direction the
   piece's argument is already leaning, which is the direction misquotations
   usually move.
2. **Silent truncation.** "and planned remediation steps" is dropped and the
   quotation is closed with a period, no ellipsis. The post presents three
   excluded items where METR listed four.

The underlying substance is true — safeguard effectiveness, the extent of the
compromise, and OpenAI's investigation process *were* out of scope, and the post
leans on that correctly in "The counterfactual nobody can check". The defect is
that the source does not contain the quotation as printed, which `specs/blog`
addresses directly: "Quotations attributed to named people MUST link a source
that contains the quotation."

## Required change

Replace that sentence with either METR's actual words, complete —

> "We agreed all other questions were also out of scope, including the
> effectiveness of safeguards, the extent of the security compromise that
> occurred, and the effectiveness of OpenAI's investigation process and planned
> remediation steps."

— or an unquoted paraphrase that keeps the agreed-ness and all four items. If
the quotation is trimmed, mark the trim. Nothing else in the post needs to move;
the argument it supports survives the correction intact and is arguably
strengthened by "We agreed".
