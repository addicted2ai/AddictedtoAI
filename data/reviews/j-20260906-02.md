## The two things the brief demanded and the entry does not carry

map and `publishes_from`. The entry carries neither. I treated that as the first
thing to check and it is **not** a defect — both omissions are correct, and I
measured both rather than reasoning about them.

(`lib/schema.mjs:209,223`) and defines no such key. Run against the real schema
(`D:/AddictedtoAI`, which has `node_modules`):


An entry following the brief literally would fail the build. `publishes_from` is
specified only in the **pending** `separate-a-claim-from-a-fact` delta, and
`grep publishes_from **/*.mjs` returns nothing — it is unimplemented.

## Checklist items

**Volatile values.** Both context windows travel as transclusions
(`{{fact:model/amazon-nova-2-lite-v1#context_window}}` vs
`{{fact:model/amazon-nova-lite-v1#context_window}}`), which is the one place the
body would have been tempted into literals — 1,000,000 against 300,000. No price
appears in prose. Every count that is not feed-bound is a dated observation with
its date in the sentence ("as observed on 5 September 2026") or in a `dated`
fact. Nothing hard-codes a value that can rot silently.

**Aliases.** `Amazon` exclusive, `AWS` shared. No other entry in the corpus
declares either name, so there is no exclusive collision, and `AWS` being
non-linkable is the right call for a name that means the cloud division rather
than the model builder — the distinction the brief asked the entry not to
collapse. `Amazon` exclusive makes it linkable; only 8 content files mention
"Amazon" at all, so the blast radius is small and each page takes at most one
link. This follows the `org/anthropic` pattern rather than the `manual` pattern
`org/nvidia` and `org/tencent` use for their over-common names, which I think is
right here: `Amazon` is the company, and the entry is the company.

**Prose adds something beyond the data.** Clearly yes, and it is the assembly
that does it rather than any single fact: a router column that looks thin next to
a host page that carries 27 other people's models; four of five listed rows
belonging to a generation the vendor's own menu has stopped naming; and a missing
top tier whose replacement is a service, not a model. None of the three is
readable off the facts table, and the third is only visible once the first two
are put side by side. It does not restate the table — the closest it comes is the
final paragraph re-quoting `portfolio` and `internal_origin`, which earns its
place by using them for the argument's conclusion rather than as a summary.

**Scope.** The diff is the entry, one proposal, and the loop's own `.job/` files.
