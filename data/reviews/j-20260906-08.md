## Aliases and the board join — the structural checks

Alias classing is sane and the derived registry agrees: `lib/aliases.mjs` makes a
name linkable only when exactly one entry declares it *and* that declaration is
`exclusive`, so `data/derived/aliases.json` correctly shows `AionLabs`
linkable:true, and `Aion Labs` / `Aion` / `Deep Forge` linkable:false. Wrapping
`Deep Forge` or the bare `Aion` in prose would be a false link; neither can
happen.

**The brief's central demand was a `feeds` map, and the entry has none. I checked
whether that costs anything and it does not — the opposite.** `lib/render/frontier.mjs`
never reads `feeds`; `matchProviders()` (line 50) joins by normalised alias
substring. I replicated that function exactly against `data/derived/catalog.json`:

```
rows: 431
normalised names >=3: [ 'aionlabs', 'aionlabs', 'aionlabs', 'aion', 'deepforge' ]
MATCHED PROVIDERS: [ 'aion-labs' ]
rows attributed to this org: 4
distinct providers in catalog: 58
providers containing "aion": [ 'aion-labs' ]
```

Exactly one provider, exactly the four catalog rows the brief names, out of 58
providers and 431 rows — no lookalike swallowed, nothing missed. The row
populates. A `feeds` map would have had nothing correct to bind to: the only two
registered sources are `openrouter-models` (rows are models, not orgs) and
`llm-releases`, no org entry in the corpus declares `feeds`, and the brief's own
rule is that a wrong binding is worse than none. Same for `publishes_from`:
`entrySchema` in `lib/schema.mjs` is `.strict()` and has no such key, and the
field appears nowhere in `lib/` — declaring it would have failed the build. Both
are carried above as corrections to the brief, not to this entry.

Also confirmed: all four `mentions` resolve to existing files under
`content/wiki/model/`; the entry is not a stub in the search index (`"b": false`,
and `lib/search-index.mjs` sets `b` from `doc.index?.stub`), which is right for a
piece with a prose body, 16 facts and 8 timeline events.
