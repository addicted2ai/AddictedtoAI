## Why approve

The brief named four readings a vanished row can have and asked for the right
one to be established from the publishers' own sources. This diff establishes
it, from two publishers, with a timestamp that lands inside the fetch window,
and then draws the consequence a reader actually needs: an integrator calling
`qwen3.8-max` across 2026-09-05 changed weights with no error, no version pin
and no signal in the numbers. It also declines to answer the one question the
sources do not settle — what became of the August 3 weights — instead of
inventing a tidy ending, which is the failure this review exists to catch. The
prose is specific and unpadded, the transclusions carry the volatile figures
rather than typing them, and nothing in it is written from intent.
