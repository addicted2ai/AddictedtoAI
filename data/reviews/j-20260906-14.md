## Why this is not `not-worth-reading`

It clears the would-send test on something it found rather than something it was
handed. Any competent newsletter can report the announcement; three of the four
sections here rest on two support pages the announcement does not link, and the

support page's three-qualifier version of the same promise — is assembly that did
not exist anywhere else at the time of writing. The fourth section asks the
question the design actually raises (what reaches Anthropic when the misuse is
the kind only the stored window reveals, and the window belongs to the misuser)
and refuses to answer it. That refusal is the piece's best sentence.
