every commit that touched `data/sources/openrouter-models/latest.json`, parses each blob, groups
them by the top-level `date`, and prints the `moonshotai/kimi-k2.5` prompt and completion prices.

```
2026-08-28 {"p":"0.0000006","c":"0.000003"}
2026-08-29 {"p":"0.0000006","c":"0.000003"}
2026-08-30 {"p":"0.0000006","c":"0.000003"}
2026-08-31 {"p":"0.00000045","c":"0.00000225"}
2026-09-01 … 2026-09-12 (12 dates) {"p":"0.00000045","c":"0.00000225"}
2026-09-14 {"p":"0.00000045","c":"0.00000225"}
```

- After 2026-08-31 there are 12 + 1 = **13** distinct dates, and every one holds

same output:
