Three things fall out of those two strings, and all three are what the diff

1. **"License" and "Weights" are the card's own field labels**, sitting under

   card's own words, which is the strongest form the fix could take.
2. **"Character for character" survives the check.** `License Open weights ·
   Meta license (weights pending)` and `Weights Not released` appear on both
   cards with identical bytes, including the `·` separator and the
   parenthetical. The claim the sentence has always made is true of the two
   lines it now names.
3. **The finding's premise holds.** "Meta has signaled open weights are
   coming." sits on the 1.2 card immediately before `Official page ↗`; at that
   same position the 1.3 card runs straight from the OpenRouter slug into
   `Official page ↗`. The third line the nearer sentence quotes is on 1.2 and
   not on 1.3, which is precisely why "the same two lines" had a false reading
   available to it. The corroborating narrative

So the ambiguity the job existed to remove is real, and the replacement text is

## Two things I checked and am NOT blocking on

**The review-hash mismatch is self-clearing.** Editing an approved entry's
prose moves its reviewed-surface hash, which `specs/review` says must report
as *mismatched* and fail `verify-launch`. That is not a defect in this diff:
`loop/lib/review.mjs:807-860` rewrites `subject:` and `reviewed:` into the
verdict record from the merged tree at merge time, so the hash rebinds to the
new bytes. Every repair to reviewed content would be unmergeable otherwise.

paragraph out of its ~76-column wrap and left `spanning two releases,` alone on
a short line. Markdown soft-wraps, so the rendered output is unaffected; I am
recording it rather than carrying it, because spending a queue item and a whole
job on reflowing one source line is out of proportion to the defect.

The one finding worth carrying forward is in the front matter above: the

about — which is exactly what `carry:` is for.
