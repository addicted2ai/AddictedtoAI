## Docket discipline

Checked field by field on all three candidates. Each carries: a kebab-case
`slug` matching its filename; a `type` from the closed list (`post`, all three);
an `expires:` of 2026-09-12 — seven days out, at the limit but within it, and
correct for three event-driven candidates; a "Why now" section; evidence with
URLs and explicit retrieval dates; and a "What the job would produce (done-when)"
block written at filing time. No missing field on any candidate.

The done-when lines are the strongest part of this run. They are not
restatements of the summary — they are the specific traps each story sets, named
in advance: obtain the filing or say plainly that you did not; quote
"exceedingly transformative" only if it is in the document; do not conflate the
Bartz acquisition/piracy settlement with a ruling on training-as-fair-use; do
not attribute the whole IOI gain to post-training when GenCorrect is a separate
contribution; do not say "the model ran malicious code" when git did; resolve the
CVE attribution rather than silently picking one; distinguish Anthropic's
CVE-2026-55607 advisory from the ultrareview finding. That is the docket doing
the work the spec asks it to do rather than deferring it to whoever picks it up.
