## Structure — does the annotation attach and render?

Not assumed; run. I wrote a script outside this worktree that imports the real
`changedFeed` from `lib/changes.mjs` and feeds it the actual `data/changes.jsonl`:

```
total feed lines: 158
index of Thomson line (0-based): 22
within home limit 24: true
annotations on it: [{"date":"2026-09-03","text":"The llm-releases description on this line — size, ...
```

So the `annotates` key resolves — `changedFeed` builds its annotation map on
exact key equality (`lib/changes.mjs:184-189`), and a typo would have produced
`ann=0` silently rather than any error. `grep -c` on the key returns 2: the
arrival at line 98 and the annotation at line 164, matching character for
character. The diff shows one `+` line and no `-` lines in `changes.jsonl`, so
append-only holds and no existing line was edited.

`lib/render/home.mjs:48-57` renders it as its own `<p class="change-annotation">`
under the mechanical line, which is what specs/loop asks for — attached, not
merged.

Worth stating plainly since it is the thin margin this job's output lives on: the
line is at index **22 of a limit of 24**. It renders today. It is also the basis
of the proposal above.

## Why approve

It does the one thing it was asked to do, at the one line it named, without
touching the mechanical record — which is the whole discipline of this job type.
Every number in it survived a fetch of the document the annotation names, and the
"still says it on 2026-09-03" claim, which is the perishable one and the one a
job could most easily have asserted from the brief instead of checking, is true
against the live page today. The two things I found are precision, not substance,
and are carried rather than blocked.
