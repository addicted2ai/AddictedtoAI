Four consequences, all of which the diff gets right:

1. The four Opus figures are written exactly as `formatPrice` renders them, so
   the prose now agrees byte-for-byte with what the entry pages, catalog and
   changed feed show after 4a33636.
2. **"six times the base" survives.** 30/5 = 6 and 150/25 = 6 exactly; the
   `5.999999999999999` above is float representation of 0.00015/0.000025, not a
   real discrepancy. Independent corroboration: the `repaid` note in
   `data/price-attribution-debt.json` re-derived "Opus 4.7-fast 6x" from live
   per-provider endpoint lists on 2026-08-31 and found it holds at the vendor's
   own rate too.
3. The two *current* Nemotron figures ($0.60, $2.40) also match `formatPrice`
   exactly. Only the two *historical* figures are written unrounded, and the
   directive's reasoning for that is confirmed by the run above: `formatPrice`
   would render them $0.63 and $3.13, turning "fell from $0.625 to $0.60" into
   a sentence about a 4% cut that reads like a 4.8% one. Since those historical
   values no longer render anywhere on the site, the unrounded form contradicts
   nothing.
4. The premise that a transclusion is impossible here is true, not assumed:
   `data/derived/feed-rows.json` carries
   `"anthropic/claude-opus-4.7-fast": {"$as_of": null, "$status": null, "$vanished": true}`
   — no pricing key exists to bind to.
