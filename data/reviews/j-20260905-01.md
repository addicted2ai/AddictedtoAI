## Judgment

The brief asked the author to tell four readings apart — retired, renamed,
folded into a tier, or delisted by one router — from the vendor's and the feed
publisher's own sources, and to say so rather than guess if the sources did not
settle it. The page does both halves. It settles what is settleable with
evidence I could reproduce, and then it stops: "Which provider carried 4.1 8B
before it went, and why it stopped, neither party publishes." That last
paragraph is the part that would have been easiest to invent and it is the part
that refuses to. The prose earns each negative — not retired because the weights
are still published, not renamed because the two rows coexisted and 4.2
finetunes 4.1's base, no deprecation because the router's own embedded data says
`null` — rather than asserting them.
