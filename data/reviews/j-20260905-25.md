## On the prose itself

It earns the `would-cite` answer above. Two things in particular are more than
the data restated. First, the asymmetry it draws deliberately: the test is
"can you source that an organisation exists", not "is this person an individual",
and it says why — one is a checkable statement about this repository's evidence,
the other is a claim about a living person, which is the thing the absent
`person` kind exists to avoid. That is a real distinction and I have not seen it
made here before. Second, it declines to rule on four of the eight the directive
handed it, and names the reason each row points the other way. The directive
asserted all eight were "individuals or personal handles"; the evidence supports
four. Contradicting the instruction that commissioned it, with the rows quoted,
is the correct behaviour and the diff is better for it.

The OpenRouter section is the same kind of correction: the directive said
OpenRouter "should certainly have an entry" as though it had none, and the
ruling establishes that it has two, of other kinds, and that what is actually
missing is `org/openrouter` because the board joins orgs. That is a real
finding, not a restatement.
