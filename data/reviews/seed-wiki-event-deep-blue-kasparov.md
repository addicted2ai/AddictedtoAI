Two defects, both minor and neither load-bearing:
1. **"Three weeks later" should be four.** The aftermath material is TWIC 135,
   9 June 1997; the closing press conference and game six were 11 May 1997 (TWIC
   131, 12 May). That is 29 days. No anchor makes it three — TWIC 134 (2 June)
   carries none of the three items. The word does no argumentative work, since
   the point is "already", but it is wrong.
2. **TWIC 132 and TWIC 135 are quoted and never cited.** Neither appears in any
   `facts[].source_url` or `timeline[].source_url`, and neither is linked inline,
   so two direct quotations and the entire aftermath paragraph are unsourced on
   the page. Both are named above for whoever adds them.
