## Verifying the finding myself

The carried finding is a hypothesis, so I replayed the primary artefact rather than reading the author's account of it. A throwaway script walked `git log --follow` over `data/sources/openrouter-models/latest.json` and parsed every blob (`rows` is an object keyed by model id, not an array). Nine distinct fetches, 2026-08-28T18:19:35.242Z through 2026-09-05T06:00:04.599Z.

Between the 2026-09-04 and 2026-09-05 fetches, rows carrying a number at `benchmarks.artificial_analysis.intelligence_index` went 164 → 52. Of the ids scored on both days, **50 went down, 0 up, 0 level**, and 114 lost the number entirely. The two rows scored on 09-05 but not on 09-04 are `openai/gpt-6-astra` and `openai/gpt-6-astra:batch`, both 54.7 — new listings. That is why the page's phrase "every other row that kept a score through it" is exactly right and "every other row" would have been wrong. Drops across the fifty run 5.7 to 11.6 points, and this row's own 58.7 → 47.1 is 11.6, the largest of them.

The four rows the finding names, measured: `google/gemini-3.8-flash` 58.7 on 09-03 and 09-04, then 47.1; `google/gemini-3.7-flash` 56.0 → 45.2; `openai/gpt-5.6-sol` 60.9 → 51.3; `x-ai/grok-4.6` 60.9 → 50.6. So the three collisions the reviewer of j-20260905-13 reported all hold on today's numbers, and on the scale the split was read on they dissolve — 59 against a 58.7 headline, "up 3" against 58.7 − 56.0 = 2.7. That is the evidence that the split is a pre-rebase reading rather than a wrong one, and it is what makes attribution the right repair and deletion the wrong one.

**One correction to the author's report, which did not reach the page.** It states that "every earlier day-to-day step in that history has 0 down and 0 up." It does not: between the 09-03 and 09-04 fetches exactly one row moved, `qwen/qwen3.8-max` 58.1 → 53.4. This strengthens rather than weakens the conclusion — a single row re-scoring is ordinary, fifty rows moving one direction on one day is not — but the record should say what the history actually contains.
