## Scope, and the vocabulary

The diff is the six tagged posts, the evidence pair, the check script that
produced the transcript, one proposal, and the loop's own `.job/` files. That is
the stated outcome — backfill the three keys, record each decline with its
reason — plus the one proposal the brief permits, and nothing else. No reserved
path is touched. `domains` was assigned only where the post's own subject is the
domain (a video model's licence, a competitive-programming model's IOI run), and
the record explains in the vocabulary's own terms why four flags carry none:
absence is K38's unmarked "general", `cybersecurity` is not a value, and reading
`agents` off the phrase "without a person guiding each step" would be the
inference `lib/domains.mjs` forbids. I agree with that restraint; inventing a
value or stretching one would have been the easier and worse move.
