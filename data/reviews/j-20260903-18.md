## The thing the fix was supposed to prevent

Before: "The threshold, in OpenAI's words, means that "with the right tools and
access, it can find…"" — nearest antecedent to `it` is "The threshold", giving
the reading that a Preparedness Framework tier finds zero-days. After: the
subject is named inside the quote, and no reading of the sentence attributes
the capability to the threshold. The frame ("The threshold … means that
[Astra] can find") now mirrors the source's own construction, in which meeting
the threshold is what "means" the model can do this. The finding is fixed at
the level it was raised.
