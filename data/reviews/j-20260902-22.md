## The source check

The claim the changed sentence now makes is that the licence's own word is
"reasonably" and that it attaches to Z.AI's determination of scope and method.
I fetched the primary document rather than trusting the post's own blockquote:

```
curl -sL https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3/raw/main/LICENSE  → 4263 bytes
```

Line 10 of that file, in full:

> If the Licensee or any of its affiliates operates a Model as a Service
> business, and the aggregate revenue of the Licensee and its affiliates
> exceeds 10 billion US dollars (or the equivalent in other currencies) in
> total over any consecutive 12 months, the Licensee must pass Z.AI's security
> review before using the Software or its derivative works for any commercial
> purpose. The scope and method of the security review shall be reasonably
> determined by Z.AI.

So:

- The word quoted in the lede, "reasonably", is in the licence, and it modifies
  `determined` — the verb governing scope and method. The repaired sentence
  reads the adverb where the licence puts it. The pre-repair wording
  ("are reasonably Z.AI's to determine") put it on the fact of Z.AI deciding,
  which is the drift the carried finding named, and it is gone.
- "subject only to" is defensible and not overclaimed: it is the sole qualifier
  in that sentence, and the post's own next section already establishes the
  negative case in its own words — "The licence names no criteria, no timeline,
  no fee, no appeal, and no mechanism by which Z.AI learns a licensee crossed
  the line."
- The post's verbatim blockquote (lines 19 and 21 of the content file) matches
  lines 9 and 10 of the fetched LICENSE character for character, including
  "(e.g., via API)", "(a)"/"(b)", and "10 billion US dollars (or the equivalent
  in other currencies)". The lede paraphrase and the quote two paragraphs down
  now say the same thing, which was the whole point of the repair.
