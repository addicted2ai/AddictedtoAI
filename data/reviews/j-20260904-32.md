## Scope

The outcome named one sentence and one file to retire, and the diff is that and
nothing else — no adjacent tidying of the annotation's other clauses, no touch on
the mechanical arrival row, no edit to `data/derived/queue.json` (correctly: the

retirement section requires.

The one thing I weighed as a possible `spec-violation` and decided against:
`specs/loop:31` says `data/changes.jsonl` "stays append-only", and this rewrites a
line. Reading it in place, that clause is a constraint on how an `interpret` job
emits — the annotation is a *new* line rather than a mutation of the mechanical
row — and this diff honours that reading exactly: the mechanical arrival is
untouched, only the loop's own commentary on it moved. Merged precedent agrees
(dffa630, an approved repair rewriting an annotation's `text` in place on
2026-09-04). Appending a second annotation instead would have left the reader two
overlapping paragraphs on one row, one of them known-imprecise, which is worse.
The genuine ambiguity in the spec text is the proposal above, not a defect here.
