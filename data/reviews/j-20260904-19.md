## The ~30%, at the source, in the raw bytes

The carried finding and this job both attribute "~30%" to
`https://llm-releases.com/models/gemini-3-8-flash`, which is the entry's own
`source_url` for `output_tokens_per_task` and `cost_per_task`. I did not
inherit the previous pass's fetch. Two checks, both 2026-09-04:

WebFetch of that URL returned, verbatim:

> "a ~30% rise in average output tokens per task (to ~48k) lifts cost per task
> to ~$0.58 at high reasoning despite unchanged per-token pricing"

WebFetch's extractor is not evidence on its own, so I fetched the raw HTML and
searched it for `30%`, `48k` and `0.58` — every occurrence, not a truncated
head. The sentence is in the served bytes:

```
$ node -e "fetch('https://llm-releases.com/models/gemini-3-8-flash')..."
--- 2260 "...with cached input keeping a 90% discount; a ~30% rise in average
output tokens per task (to ~48k) lifts cost per task to ~$0.58 at high
reasoning despite unchanged per-token pricing. Available in the Gemini app..."
```

**The page carries two different ~30% figures and they are not the same
claim.** The first occurrence in the document (offset 1715) is
"52 at low (matching Gemini 3.6 Flash at ~30% lower cost per task)" — a
*cost* comparison against the 3.6 row at low reasoning. The one this diff uses
is the second (offset 2260) — a *token* rise. A grep that stopped at the first
hit would have attached the wrong ~30% to this sentence. It didn't happen here:
the edited prose reads "average output tokens per task rose ~30%, to ~48k,
lifting cost per task to ~$0.58 at high reasoning", which is the second
sentence's structure, clause for clause — rise on the tokens, "at high
reasoning" on the cost. That is the correct one of the two.

The source states the rise without naming what it rose from, and the entry's
prose reproduces that same silence rather than inventing a baseline. Correct
call: the surrounding paragraph makes the 3.7 comparison, but the figure itself
is not attributed further than the source attributes it.

The RSS snapshot the Pulse holds
(`data/sources/llm-releases/latest.json`, item
`bd089f31-3f2e-40d7-9596-71c3d8c81216`) does **not** contain the ~30%, the
~48k or the ~$0.58 — its `description` stops at the price line. So the page,
not the snapshot, is the only support for this sentence, which is what the
prose cites and what I fetched.
