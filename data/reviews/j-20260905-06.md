## The instrument problem, first, because everything below depends on it

The declared anchor is a 263 KB court PDF. WebFetch cannot read it. Handed the
anchor URL it answered:

> I cannot locate the specific phrase "Training Of LLMs On Written Works Is
> Exceedingly Transformative" in this PDF document. ... I'm unable to reliably
> extract the complete sentence beginning with "In sum, the use of copies to
> train LLMs" or identify the exact filing date and case number from the
> provided content.

That is the same answer it would give for a document that did not contain the
quote, so it is evidence of nothing. I wrote my own extractor rather than treat
it as a finding.

First attempt (parenthesised text literals plus raw hex, no CMap resolution)
returned:

```
objects=394 objstm=2 pages=20 chars=11898 empty_pages=0
```

and its output was junk — page-header stamps and marked-content language tags:

```
en-UST
en-USYOR
K
...
Case 1:25-cv-03483-SHS-OTW     Document 316     Filed 09/01/26     Page 1 of 20
```

Exit 0, twenty pages, no errors, and not one word of the brief. This is exactly
the failure mode the accompanying proposal names, and I reproduced it
independently before reading the proposal's account of it.

Working version: expand the two `/Type /ObjStm` containers, resolve each page's
`/Resources /Font` map, parse the referenced `/ToUnicode` CMaps (`beginbfchar`
and both `beginbfrange` forms), strip `<<...>>` marked-content property dicts so
their literals are not mistaken for show-text operands, then decode `<hex>` and
`(literal)` operands through the active font:

```
objects=394 objstm=2 pages=20 chars=46484 empty_pages=0
```

46,484 characters, all twenty pages non-empty. Every quotation below was checked
against that text with a whitespace- and quote-normalising substring search.
Font-run splits are real and I allowed for them: the PDF renders "improperly
collapse d", "the large st technology companies", "s he published a piece".

Downloaded with `curl -sS -L -w "%{http_code} %{size_download}"` →
`200 269531`, matching the 263.2 KB the proposal reports.
