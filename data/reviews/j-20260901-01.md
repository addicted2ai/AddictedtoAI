**Spec check on the repair shape.** `openspec/specs/wiki/spec.md:170` ("A
vanished row cannot pose as current") requires that *while* an entry declares a
row the snapshot no longer contains, the bound facts render last-known values
with an as-of date and a repair finding enters the queue. That is the safety
net's behaviour, not a prohibition on resolving it — this job is the repair the
scenario's second half asks for. The shape used here (status active→retired,
maintenance living→dormant, feeds and feed-bound facts removed, one dated
sourced timeline row) is the same shape as `de51f54`
(`model/arcee-ai-virtuoso-large`, 2026-08-29), which itself followed
`j-20260829-03`'s olmo entry. No spec text requires preserving the last-known
price after the repair, and preserving it would mean writing a `cited` fact
whose `source_url` is a catalog that no longer contains the row — a citation
that does not support its own value. Dropping the facts is the more honest of
the two options.

**The `link_check` movement in `data/derived/freshness.json`** (`checked`
25→0, `due` 40→15) is not a content claim and is not a defect introduced by this
diff: it is what the machinery's own shared derivation produces. `rederive()` is
documented at `pulse/lib/rederive.mjs:44-46` as always calling `rollingLinkCheck`
"with `offline: true` by default, which returns the correct shape while touching
no network", and the Desk runs that same function after every merge. `grep`ing
`link_check` across every `.mjs` in the worktree returns only
`pulse/lib/freshness.mjs`, `pulse/lib/rederive.mjs` and two test files — nothing
renders these counts to a reader, so no page states a falsehood in the interim
before the next scheduled Pulse restores real counts.

**Scope.** The diff touches the one target entry, the four derived files that
are pure functions of it, and the `.job/` brief and source files the loop itself
writes. Nothing wider. `data/config.json`, `runners.yml` and `openspec/specs/`
are untouched.
