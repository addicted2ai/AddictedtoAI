## Is anything now inconsistent with the rest of the page

No — and this is the question the edit could plausibly have broken, so I looked at it directly rather than reasoning from the diff's size.

Two screens above, the post says the authors "run their general pipeline on the same problems five times ... That mean is 521.72, with an observed range of 495.0 to 545.8." That is IOI **2026**, post-competition. The repaired sentence is the general pipeline on IOI **2025**. Different problem sets, different experiments, both correctly labelled means. The edit removes an inconsistency rather than creating one: before it, the post was scrupulous about the 2026 mean and silent about the 2025 means, which is exactly what the carried finding said. What it does create is a readability near-collision, carried above as the first two entries — not a contradiction.

The other thing worth keeping apart is the final-round budget. The paper expands the last GenCorrect round to 1,000 solutions, but only in §5.2's competition-specific adaptations ("In the final round, we increase the generation budget to 1,000 solutions"); the general pipeline the repaired sentence describes is 200 generations narrowed to 10 submissions per round. The post keeps those on the right side of the line, and the repair did not disturb it.
