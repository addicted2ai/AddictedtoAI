## What I fetched, and what the bytes said

each fragment is present in the retrieved text, rather than trusting WebFetch's
extractor. That mattered: WebFetch's summary of The Hacker News rendered the

"posts, across more than one site" gloss look unsupported. The bytes say
otherwise.
