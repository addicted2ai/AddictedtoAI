## The blocking defect — `"Daybreak"` is classed `exclusive`

`specs/wiki`, Requirement "Aliases are registered, classed, and collision-safe":

> `manual` — never automatically linked by any process. **Single common words
> and bare brand tokens** (`"Claude"`, `"Gemini"`, `"Llama"`) **MUST be
> `manual`**.

`"Daybreak"` is both: a single common English word and the bare brand token of
"OpenAI Daybreak". It is declared `exclusive`. That is a MUST clause, and the
entry classes the two *distinctive* names (`"Daybreak Blue"`, `"Daybreak Red"`)
as `manual` while giving the bare token the linkable class — precisely inverted.

I did not stop at the spec text. The measured consequence is worse than
cosmetic, because `"Daybreak"` is a whole-token substring of the other exclusive
alias `"OpenAI Daybreak"`, and `lib/linker.mjs` rule 3 refuses overlapping
candidates with no tie-break "not even when they point at the same entry". I ran
the branch's own linker over the real sentence at
`content/blog/openai-gpt-6-astra-system-card.md:56`:

```
--- AS AUTHORED: 0 link(s)
... safeguards planned "in the coming weeks" through OpenAI Daybreak for vulnerability ...

--- WITH "Daybreak" AS manual: 1 link(s)
... safeguards planned "in the coming weeks" through [[OpenAI Daybreak -> concept/openai-daybreak]] for vulnerability ...
```

**Zero links.** The entry's whole argument for existing is that three posts keep
restating these facts "with nowhere to put them" — and as authored, the post
that uses OpenAI's own phrasing "through OpenAI Daybreak" gets no link to it at
all. Both candidates overlap, both are refused, and rule 4 means the alias does
not get a second chance further down the page. On
`content/blog/openai-astra-critical-designation.md` the same pair links the word
`Daybreak` and leaves ` Blue` hanging outside the anchor, because
`AFTER_BLOCK` (`[A-Za-z0-9_\-/+#@]`) does not include a space.

I also scanned all 539 wiki entries on this branch for an entry declaring two
`exclusive` aliases where one is a whole-token substring of the other:

```
SELF-OVERLAP /concept/openai-daybreak.md :: "Daybreak" inside "OpenAI Daybreak"
entries scanned: 539 | self-overlapping exclusive pairs: 1
```

One, and it is this one. This is not corpus practice being followed; the spec's
own worked example points the other way, classing `"Claude Opus 5"` exclusive
and the shorter `"Opus 5"` shared.

### The required change

**One line.** In `content/wiki/concept/openai-daybreak.md`, change the
`"Daybreak"` alias from `class: exclusive` to `class: manual`. `"OpenAI
Daybreak"` stays `exclusive` and becomes the entry's single linkable name,
which is what the measurement above shows restores the link. `"Daybreak Blue"`
and `"Daybreak Red"` are correctly `manual` already and need no change.

The `carry:` entry above (OpenAI's "our" rendering in this site's voice) is the
second thing worth fixing while the file is open, but it is not what I am
blocking on.
