## The claim the edit makes, checked against the file itself

The clause now reads: "unlike the two earlier status flips on this source,
both of which were dropped expiration dates, not new deprecations."

Three separable assertions. I checked each against `data/changes.jsonl`
directly rather than against the brief's account of it.

**"the two earlier status flips on this source"** — there are exactly two, and
both are on `openrouter-models`. `grep -n '"field":"status"'` over the whole
file returns four lines and no more (I counted rather than piping through
`head`; the file is 145 lines, all 145 parse as JSON):

- line 91, `2026-08-31`, `moonshotai/kimi-k2.5`, `"old":"deprecated","new":"active"`

- line 114, `2026-09-01`, `nex-agi/nex-n2-pro`, `"old":"active","new":"deprecated"`

line 113's `key` exactly), 114 is its same-day sibling, so "the two earlier"
resolves unambiguously to 86 and 91.

**"were dropped expiration dates"** — both, from their own annotations:

- line 90 (GLM 4.5V): "dropped the 2026-12-31 expiration date it carried in
  the previous snapshot"
- line 92 (Kimi K2.5): "cleared the 2026-08-31 expiration date it carried in

And both flip rows carry `"expiration_date":null` in their excerpt, which is
the mechanical confirmation independent of the prose.

## What the edit stopped asserting, and why that was the point

The old text called both flips "metadata corrections of a dropped date". Line
92's Kimi annotation, four lines above this one in the same file, says in its
own words: "It matters more than a badge: the derived catalog row now reads
`active` while the joined entry `model/moonshotai-kimi-k2-5` still declares
`status: deprecated` ... the two surfaces now disagree about the same model."
The GLM annotation on line 90 does call itself "a metadata correction on an
otherwise unchanged row" and says "It matters only for the status badge" — so
the old clause was right about one of the two and flatly contradicted by the
other, in the same file a reader would be reading. The new clause states the
shared mechanism and drops the shared significance claim. Nothing false was
replaced with something false, and the self-contradiction is gone.
