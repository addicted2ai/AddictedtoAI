## The finding

One number does not reproduce, and it is stated twice as a measurement.

`proposal.md`: *"Measured on 2026-09-05: of the 35 curated listings under
`content/directory/tools/`, **28 map to no domain at all** — `local`,
`inference`, `training`, `observability`, `data`, `frameworks` and `evaluation`
are jobs performed across every domain and belonging to none. An axis that
leaves four listings in five with nothing to say is a different axis…"*

`specs/directory/spec.md`: *"The measurement that settles it: of the 35 curated
listings under `content/directory/tools/` on 2026-09-05, **28 map to no domain
at all**. Their categories — `local`, `inference`, `training`, `observability`,
`data`, `frameworks`, `evaluation` — describe jobs that are performed across
every domain and belong to none."*

Counted from the tree, the category of every one of the 35 listings:

```
local 5 · inference 5 · training 3 · retrieval 3 · observability 3 · data 3
coding 3 · image 2 · frameworks 2 · evaluation 2 · audio 2 · agents 2   (= 35)
```

The seven categories the draft itself names as mapping to nothing hold
5+5+3+3+3+2+2 = **23** listings, not 28. The other twelve are coding 3, agents 2,
image 2, audio 2 and retrieval 3 — and `EN-domain-facet.md` §4, which the draft
cites, maps all five of those to domains (`retrieval` → `research`). So the
upstream mapping gives 23 unmapped; treating `retrieval` as unmapped, which the
delta's own text leans toward when it calls `retrieval` → `research` "a judgment
someone has to make", gives 26. There is no reading on which the answer is 28,
and the derived phrase "four listings in five" (80%) is 66% or 74% in fact.

The likely origin of the slip is visible in the arithmetic: 35 listings minus
the **seven category names** in the "none" list is 28. The error is not the
author's invention — it is in `EN-domain-facet.md` §4 and §5, DESK-ORDER-001 §3
and `SPEC-REVIEW-GUIDE.md` row 36 — but the draft restated it as its own dated
measurement rather than as a quoted upstream figure, which is why both reasons
are cited: the claim is unsupported by the data, and the sentence asserts a
measurement that was not taken. That the draft does exactly the right thing
twice elsewhere — independently re-measuring 545/544 against §3, and catching
that the "431/446" coverage figure went stale under K38 — is what makes this one
worth a revision rather than a shrug. It is the same class of check, skipped
once, on the one figure that is doing normative work: it is the sole quantitative
justification offered for making `domains` optional on tool listings, and it now
sits inside a spec delta labelled "the measurement that settles it".
