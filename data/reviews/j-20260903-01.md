## The deleted sentence was false, and I measured it rather than reasoning it

This is the substantive finding of the review, and the author got it right.

No `pricing` key, no `context_length`, no date. I then ran the real resolver

`lib/facts.mjs:269-273` turns `value == null` into the ABSENT dash and

The cause is `pulse/lib/derive.mjs:117-126`: `inLatest ?? inPrevious ?? null`,
