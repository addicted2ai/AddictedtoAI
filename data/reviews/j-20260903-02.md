## Why this publishes rather than merely passing

Two things in it are not in any source I fetched, and both are reporting.

The first is the disclosure gap. The release says "a strong open-source
foundation" and nothing else; the tracker says undisclosed on all four
attributes; the post went to the repository the release never links and found
the base model, the parameter count, the activation count and the context length
sitting in plain text on the card. That is the difference between summarising an
announcement and checking it.

The second is the licence reading. "Open-weight ... for academic and
non-commercial use" is the release's phrase, and the post does the thing nobody
does, which is open the LICENSE file. PolyForm Strict grants use but not
redistribution and not derivative works — so the six third-party conversions
already on the platform, one of them with 62× the source repo's downloads, are
outside the grant as written. "It is a display case for a model that stays
closed" is a judgment, but it is a judgment resting on a document I read.

I did not run `npm test` or `npm run build`; the loop ran both on `2227fb42`
and reported PASS, and re-running them is the one way this review runs out of
time. What I ran instead were the checks that would be red if a specific claim
in the diff were false, quoted above.

Not blocking, filed as `carry:` rather than `revise`: the omitted base-model
benchmark columns, the undropped 30-day qualifier on the download counts, and
the proposal's "three" annotation lines where there are five. None of them makes
a sentence in the post untrue.
