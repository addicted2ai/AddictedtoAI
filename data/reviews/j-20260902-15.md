## Required change 2 — the enumeration drops GLM-5.2, the one column the summary's headline claim rests on

The added sentence reads: "The footnote states its method for GLM-5.3, Kimi K3
and Qwen3.8 Max only — the Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 4.8 figures on the same
row carry no stated method, so the row is not a like-for-like leaderboard."

Nothing there is false, and the word "only" is doing real work. But the row has
eight columns and the footnote covers three, and the queue item's own title is
"the card states ExploitGym provenance for **only three of its eight columns**."
The sentence names three of the five uncovered columns and silently omits
GLM-5.2 (29/39) — which is precisely the number the proposal's summary builds
its headline on: "its ExploitGym 105/130 more than tripling GLM-5.2's 29/39."
GLM-5.3's figures were rescaled at 115 TPS; the card states no rescaling factor
for GLM-5.2 at all. A writer reading this evidence block would reasonably infer
that the like-for-like problem is confined to the three frontier models and that
the GLM-5.3-vs-GLM-5.2 comparison is clean, which is the opposite of what the
card supports. The fix is a few words: name GLM-5.2 among the figures with no
stated method (DeepSeek-V4 Pro-0813 is a dash and needs no mention).

For the record, the card's own prose is more careful than the proposal's:
line 16 claims GLM-5.3 "more than **doubles** GLM-5.2 on exploitation
benchmarks", not triples. The proposal's "more than tripling" is arithmetically
true of this row (105/29 = 3.6×, 130/39 = 3.3×) and I am not asking for it to
change — but it is the vendor's number, computed across two unstated rescalings,
stated more strongly than the vendor states it. Naming GLM-5.2 in the method
sentence is what keeps the post honest about that.
