## One thing I considered and cleared rather than raising

This diff **rewrites** an annotation line in place rather than appending a new
one, and `specs/loop:31` says "`data/changes.jsonl` stays append-only". I
checked whether that is a `spec-violation` and concluded it is not:

- The invariant that sentence protects is stated in its own parenthetical and
  in `lib/changes.mjs:16-19` — an annotation is a *separate line* keyed by
  `annotates`, never merged into the mechanical change line, so "the mechanical
  line stays exactly what the machine observed". This diff leaves every
  mechanical line untouched; only the model's own commentary moved.
- Annotations are explicitly excluded from anchoring (`lib/anchors.mjs:45-51,87`
  — "the whole point of the anchor is evidence its author cannot create"), so
  no anchor depends on the annotation's bytes, and the line carries no `key`, so
  the Pulse's append-dedupe is unaffected.
- Two of the three findings can only be cleared by an in-place edit — carry-3
  prescribes "Dropping the parenthetical", carry-2 "Adding the completion pair
  fixes it".
- It is established, merged practice. `git log -- data/changes.jsonl` shows
  `13be7be` ("apply both j-20260902-02 carries to the glm-4.5v annotation") and
  `a818eef` ("tighten real-lifecycle-event clause in nex-n2-pro annotation"),
  both **1 insertion, 1 deletion** on this exact file.

The backticks in the annotation render as literal backtick characters to a
visitor (the feed emits plain escaped text, not Markdown). That predates this
