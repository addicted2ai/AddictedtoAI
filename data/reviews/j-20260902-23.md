> If the Licensee or any of its affiliates operates a Model as a Service

> total over any consecutive 12 months, the Licensee must pass Z.AI's security
> review before using the Software or its derivative works for any commercial

The new paragraph's load-bearing claim is that "the trigger is not tied to
GLM-5.3" and that the condition names "no mention of the Software". That is
what the file says. "the Software" appears in the sentence exactly once, in
the consequence ("before using the Software or its derivative works"), never
in either limb of the antecedent. The first limb is a MaaS-business test with
no object; the second is a revenue test on the licensee-plus-affiliates group.
Neither is qualified by which model the MaaS business runs. The definitional
sentence above it is generic in the same way — "giving a third party access to
language model inference or fine-tuning (e.g., via API)", not access to the
Software.

So the two consequences the paragraph draws both hold on the text:

- a $10B group whose MaaS business runs on another model is inside the first
  limb — nothing in the sentence excludes it;
- that group, if it then fine-tunes GLM-5.3 internally for a commercial
  purpose, needs the review before doing so, because the consequence attaches
  to "any commercial purpose" and not to MaaS use.

The second one depends on the post's earlier section ("The condition attaches
to all commercial use, not just MaaS"), which is itself correct against the
same sentence, so the new paragraph is not resting on an unsupported step.
