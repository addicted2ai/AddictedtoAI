## Aliases — measured, not reasoned

`concept/covered-models` declares "Covered Models" and "Covered Model" both
`exclusive`, and the shorter is a prefix of the longer. That is close to the
shape `data/proposals/detect-self-overlapping-exclusive-aliases.md` describes,
where two overlapping exclusive aliases on one entry make it silently
unlinkable under linker rule 3 ("overlapping candidates are all dropped — no
tie-break, not even when they point at the same entry"). So I ran the real
linker rather than reasoning about it:

```
"Anthropic designates Covered Models on that page."
   links made: ["Covered Models"]
"Fable 5.1 is a Covered Model as of August."
   links made: ["Covered Model"]
"A Covered Model designation applies to Covered Models generally."
   links made: ["Covered Model","Covered Models"]
```

No refusal. `AFTER_BLOCK = /[A-Za-z0-9_\-/+#@]/` (lib/linker.mjs:47) blocks
"Covered Model" from matching inside "Covered Models" because the next
character is `s`, so the two aliases never produce overlapping candidates. The
classing is sound and this entry is not an instance of that proposal's defect —
worth recording, because the pair *looks* like one on inspection.
