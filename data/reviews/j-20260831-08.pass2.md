## Form, and the finish line for it

A **news note**. It declares an `anchor:` rather than a derivation method,
leads with a dated event, and names who it lands on — three parties,
concretely: recipients (reply, and an incorrect or absent reply is its own
violation), unasked providers with no published summary (you match the second
set's criterion), and open-weight shippers relying on the carve-out (it does
not cover the summary). A note's finish line is that an affected reader knows
what happened, what changes for them, and where the primary evidence is. All
three are met, the third now genuinely.

The traps the brief named are still avoided after the rewrite: the post does
not imply the RFIs targeted open-weight releases (the recipient question and
the obligation question stay in separate sections), it states plainly that an
RFI "is an information request, not a finding, a charge or a fine," and the
incidents line is now explicitly marked non-causal rather than merely left
alone. The title claims no more than the body proves — both of its clauses are
sourced, the second to the Q&A.
