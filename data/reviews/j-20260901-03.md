## The repair shape

`status: active → retired`, `maintenance: living → dormant`, the `feeds:`
binding and its four bound facts removed, one dated sourced timeline row added.
That is byte-for-byte the shape merged for `mistralai/codestral-2508-batch`
(0f48167) and the two entries before it, so it is the established convention
rather than an invention here. I checked the spec for a rule the removal might
violate: `openspec/specs/wiki/spec.md:170` ("A vanished row cannot pose as
current") describes the pre-repair *rendering*, and `specs/pulse` describes the
queue derivation — neither prescribes what the repair does, and neither is
contradicted. I do think the shape loses something real; that is the proposal
above, not a defect in this diff, and it is not this job's to fix.
