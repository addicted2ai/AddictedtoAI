## What has to change

**1. The trigger drops "or any of its affiliates", and one sentence is wrong
because of it.** Clause 2's second paragraph opens: *"If the Licensee **or any
of its affiliates** operates a Model as a Service business, and the aggregate
revenue of the Licensee and its affiliates exceeds 10 billion US dollars ...
the Licensee must pass Z.AI's security review."* Two conditions, and the first
one reaches affiliates. The post carries the affiliate reach on the revenue
test — "the aggregate revenue ... over the licensee and its affiliates
together" — and then loses it on the MaaS test in three places:

- the lede: "commercial use by any licensee **running** Model-as-a-Service";
- the analysis: "The trigger is operating a MaaS business", closing with **"A
  company over the line that never serves inference to third parties is
  untouched by the clause"**;
- "Who it lands on": "**If you run hosted inference on GLM-5.3** and your
  group's revenue over any trailing 12 months is $10B or more".

The bolded sentence is contradicted by the text it cites. A $10B+ group whose
subsidiary operates a MaaS business, and which serves no inference itself, is
a licensee whose commercial use is conditioned — that is the whole reason the
clause says "or any of its affiliates" rather than "the Licensee". The post's
own reading in the next paragraph makes this worse, not better: since the
precondition attaches to "any commercial purpose", the affiliate route is how
a company with no MaaS product at all ends up inside the gate. This is the
post's central section and the one thing a reader would act on, so it is the
blocking change: state the trigger as the licence states it, and either drop
the "untouched" sentence or qualify it ("a company over the line whose group
runs no MaaS business at all").

**2. "Reasonably" is dropped from both paraphrases.** The licence: "The scope
and method of the security review shall be **reasonably** determined by Z.AI."
The lede: "The review's scope and method are Z.AI's to set." The done-when
line the post is answering: "the review's scope is Z.AI's to set". The
blockquote shows the reader the real word, which keeps this from being a
fabrication, but a post whose subject is the exact reach of a clause should
not shed the one word limiting the licensor's discretion. Keep "reasonably
determined" in the paraphrase; if the point is that the standard is
unelaborated, the post can say that — it already says, correctly, that the
licence names no criteria, timeline, fee or appeal.

Everything else in the diff checked out. The piece is worth publishing once
those are fixed; the reporting under it is solid, and the revision is two
sentences of work, not a rewrite.
