## What the finding asked for, and what the schema allows

The carry named two closes: "Adding the press release alongside, or dropping the
date qualifier from `open_weights`". The first is not available for a timeline
row. `lib/schema.mjs:201-207`:

```
const timelineEvent = z
  .object({
    date: isoDate,
    event: z.string().min(1, 'must not be empty'),
    source_url: httpUrl,
  })
  .strict();
```

One `source_url`, `.strict()`, so a row cannot carry a second citation
"alongside" anything. For a dated row the only shape that satisfies the finding
is a single document that states the event *and* the day. That is what the
author did, and it is the right reading of a finding whose literal first
suggestion the schema forbids.
