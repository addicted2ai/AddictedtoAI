  `"$as_of":"2026-09-01","$status":"deprecated","$vanished":false`.
- `lib/build-content.mjs:152` —
  `doc.currentStatus = doc.hasBody ? doc.data.status : currentStatusOf(doc, factCtx(doc));`
  The entry file ends at its closing `---` with no prose, and
  `lib/corpus.mjs:100` sets `hasBody: hasProse(body)`, so this is the stub
  branch; `currentStatusOf` (`lib/facts.mjs:176-189`) finds the
  `field: 'status'` fact, resolves it, and returns the feed value in preference

- `data/derived/catalog.json`, the row with
  `"entry_id":"model/nex-agi-nex-n2-pro"`, carries
  `"expiration_date":"2026-09-04","status":"deprecated"`.

The annotation's phrasing survives this: it attributes the rendered value to
the feed-bound fact and calls the entry a stub, which is exactly the
distinction (`hasBody`) that makes the sentence true. Had it said the entry's
`status:` field was `deprecated`, it would have been false.
