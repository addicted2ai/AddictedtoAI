## The two front-matter absences, checked rather than taken on trust

The brief demands a `feeds` map and `publishes_from`, and the diff has neither.
I checked both rather than treating the omission as a violation:

- `publishes_from` **cannot be declared**. `entrySchema` in `lib/schema.mjs:209`
  is `.strict()` and its key set is exactly `id, kind, display_name, aliases,
  status, maintenance, feeds, facts, timeline, mentions, themes`. The string
  `publishes_from` does not occur anywhere under `lib/` (grep, zero matches) —
  it lives only in the in-flight `separate-a-claim-from-a-fact` delta. Declaring
  it would fail the build. Omitting it is correct.
- `feeds` is not the join the board uses. No org entry in the corpus carries
  one (grep for `^feeds:` across all 22 files in `content/wiki/org/`, zero
  matches), and `lib/render/frontier.mjs:50` joins orgs to catalog providers by
  normalised alias containment. `norm("inclusionAI")` is `"inclusionai"`, which
  equals the catalog provider id exactly, so this entry's row populates. The
  brief's premise is wrong about the mechanism; the entry is not. That
  discrepancy is what the proposal above is for.

Not a `scope-violation`: the diff is one content file plus the loop's own
`.job/` artifacts.

## Aliases and the prose bar

Aliases are classed sanely. The registry keys on the raw name
(`lib/aliases.mjs`, no normalisation on the claim key), so `inclusionAI` and
`InclusionAI` are two distinct exclusive claims rather than a collision, and
both are the org's own name in the two casings that appear in the wild — the
site itself uses lowercase-i, Hugging Face uses capital-I. `IAI` as `manual` is
the right call: it is a three-letter string the org's own about page introduces,
and auto-linking it in prose would be a menace.

The prose earns its place and does not restate the table. Its throughline —
API first, weights second, and the interval is shrinking — is derived from
dates that are scattered across four different services and assembled here for
the first time, which is the "scattered things assembled" clause of
`specs/editorial` met literally. Ling-3.0-tiny as the deliberate
counter-example, the community GGUF out-downloading the vendor's own by 622×,
and the closing turn to LLaDA are all judgments the data does not make by
itself. No filler openers, no unsupported superlatives, no self-reference.
Voice is plain and the awe is in the subject. Were it not for the two false
statements above, this would be an `approve`.
