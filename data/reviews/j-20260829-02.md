# Two things I noted and did not block on

Both concern the **entry**, which this diff does not modify, and in both cases
the evidence file quotes the source's fuller wording verbatim right next to the
front-matter value, so nothing is concealed from a later reader:

- `former_name` says "Zhipu AI, until the 2025 rebranding"; the source says
  formerly known as Zhipu AI **outside China**. The qualifier matters — the
  Chinese-market name is a separate question — and the entry states it flatly.
- `listing` asserts "the first major Chinese LLM company to IPO"; the source
  hedges it as "It is **considered to be** China's first major LLM company that
  went through an IPO."

Marking these "matches" is defensible (attributive hedging is Wikipedia's house
style, and the substance is what the page asserts), and neither was introduced
by this job, so neither is a `false-or-unsupported-claim` against this diff. I
record them because a re-verification pass is the natural place to catch a
value that outruns its source, and a future upkeep job on this entry should
tighten both.

Separately, worth knowing rather than fixing: three of the five facts are
classed `static` (`founded`, `former_name`) or `dated` (`listing`), which the
wiki spec defines as never re-checked. Only `headquarters` and
`weights_license` are `slow` (120 days), and neither was due. The job re-checked
all five because the directive said five; that is over-delivery, not a defect.
