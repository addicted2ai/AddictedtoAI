Clause 2, second paragraph, as returned on 2026-09-02:

> If the Licensee or any of its affiliates operates a Model as a Service
> business, and the aggregate revenue of the Licensee and its affiliates
> exceeds 10 billion US dollars (or the equivalent in other currencies) in
> total over any consecutive 12 months, the Licensee must pass Z.AI's security
> review before using the Software or its derivative works for any commercial
> purpose. The scope and method of the security review shall be reasonably
> determined by Z.AI.

Against that text, sentence by sentence:

- **Affiliate reach on the MaaS test — restored and correct.** The clause is
  two conditions joined by "and": (i) the Licensee **or any of its
  affiliates** operates a MaaS business, (ii) the aggregate revenue of the
  Licensee **and its affiliates** exceeds $10B over any consecutive 12
  months. The post now carries the affiliate reach on both, in all three
  places pass 1 flagged.
- **"A company over the line whose group runs no MaaS business at all is
  untouched" — now true.** With condition (i) failing across the whole
  affiliate group, the conjunction fails and the review precondition never
  attaches. The old sentence ("never serves inference to third parties
  itself") was false against exactly the "or any of its affiliates" the
  clause spends words on; the new one is the correct form of the same point.
- **"A subsidiary's MaaS business triggers the parent's condition too" — the
  clause's own logic.** A subsidiary is an affiliate; condition (i) is
  satisfied by the affiliate's business, and the obligation ("the Licensee
  must pass Z.AI's security review") lands on the Licensee.
- **"reasonably" — restored.** The word is in the licence and is now in the
  paraphrase. Placement is awkward enough that I have carried it above, not
  wrong enough to block: the verbatim quote sits two paragraphs below and
  shows the reader the real sentence. The Chinese half of the same file says
  the same thing — "安全审查的范围和方式由Z.AI合理确定" — so nothing turns on
  which language you read.
