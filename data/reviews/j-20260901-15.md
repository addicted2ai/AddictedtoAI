## Is `status: deprecated` on this page legal, or is it an entry fighting its feed?

This is the part I most expected to be a defect, and it is not. The rule lives
in `lib/build-content.mjs:152`:

```js
doc.currentStatus = doc.hasBody ? doc.data.status : currentStatusOf(doc, factCtx(doc));
```

An entry with a prose body keeps its authored `status:`; a stub takes the
resolved feed value. The comment immediately above it (lines 136–151) names
*this exact entry* as its worked example, in these words: its body "argues,
sourced, that Moonshot's own retirement notice outweighs a feed row whose
expiry OpenRouter has since cleared — forcing the feed's answer onto that page
would silently overrule a reviewed, cited judgment with an unreviewed one."
The page as it stands is what that machinery documents; it is not an
unexplained override.

I also checked what the `status` fact itself renders, since the entry binds
`path: $status` to the feed while the badge says `deprecated`. `resolveFact`
returns state `feed` with value `active` there, so the facts table shows the
router's answer beside a `deprecated` badge — the disagreement is visible on
the page rather than papered over, which is the entry's point.
