## The editorial question

The finding this repairs was that the page answered "can I call this?" for two
of three routes and left the third to inference while printing its price. It
now answers the third in the only honest form available: the listing is there,
the price is there, and the page declines to say the call returns anything.
"this page does not assert" is self-referential, but the corpus already writes
that move where a claim outruns what was measured —
`content/learn/open-weights-and-closed-models.md:70` ("this page does not
settle it"), `content/tutorials/chat-template-is-not-the-model.md:419` ("an
assumption this page does not test"). It is a convention here, not a tic.

Placement is right too: the clause lands in the paragraph about the
router/vendor disagreement, immediately after "The router's data stopped
carrying a date; the vendor's page says the model is discontinued", so the
antecedent of "The row" is unambiguous, and it lands *before* the paragraph
about weights staying downloadable — which is the third route. The three
routes now read in order.
