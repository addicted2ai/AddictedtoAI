**I measured the pagination myself rather than inheriting it**, because the
whole repair is that one arithmetic claim. I fetched the anchor PDF
(`http 200 bytes 269531`, matching the 263.2 KB on record), inflated its 71
FlateDecode streams and worked from the decoded text, not from WebFetch's prose.

1. The ECF stamps are their own tiny overlay streams, one per page, with the

satisfies the finding as stated in its title ("Cite a source that actually names

> "Altman has discussed the stake sale with Trump, Commerce Secretary Howard

That is character-for-character the sentence the post quotes, including "the FT

The post's Sources row is careful in the way this corpus asks for: it names the
wire, names the outlet it was read via, and says what that link is for. That is
the "quote the document you name" rule applied to a syndication, and it is the
right handling.
