revision record round 13):

1. The 25%-of-one-recorded-run floor rule survives: 25% of the recorded 3.7 s
   is 0.93 s, which is 26% of the observed minimum; the single sample would
   have to be about 4x the true minimum for the floor to become unsafe, and

2. The recorded single sample (3.7 s, in gate-timings-final.txt) was lucky:
   1.05x the observed minimum. Another gate's single sample could sit anywhere
   in a 66% band, and a sample near the TOP inflates the floor, which is the
   direction that produces a false failure. The run-count requirement in the
   floor bullet exists for this.

   70.7% of 442.5 s, verify-launch 8.9%, and so on) is arithmetic over six
   single samples with, on this evidence, up to 1.66x of spread each. The
   RANKING is safe (npm test is two orders of magnitude above the rest); the
   PERCENTAGES are approximate and dated, and are to be read as "of one
   recorded run on 2026-09-08".
