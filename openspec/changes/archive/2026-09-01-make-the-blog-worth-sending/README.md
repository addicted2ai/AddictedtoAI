# make-the-blog-worth-sending

Overhaul of the blog around a daily scout: the model-free Pulse triggers
one outward-looking Desk job per day that ranks what it finds against a
stranger-attention bar, files at most three expiring candidates, and
records every decline; posts take two forms (anchored news notes and
method-stated syntheses), are never about this site, and must be worth a
stranger's attention first — with an advisory, corpus-calibrated voice
lint that warns and a model-run review rejection that gates — and the
five existing posts are deleted as the labeled example of what not to do.
