## 2. THE SWEEP — every trial that reads or extracts source, including any the review did not find

Method: a script under the OS temp area split the suite into its 39 `test(` blocks and flagged any block holding `../run.mjs`, `readFileSync(`, `new Function`, or `indexOf` aimed at the shipped file, plus any block spawning the shipped program via `runPulse` and any block injecting reader fakes. Full output was read with no truncation and no count limit. Result: 39 blocks, 39 collected in every suite run.

- Exactly two blocks read `pulse/run.mjs` as text: the display-edge trial (reads the file, extracts the threshold literal by regex, locates `formatAheadDuration` by `indexOf`, scans for a balanced brace, evaluates the bytes through `new Function`) and the stat-failed guard-text trial (reads the file, asserts three substrings). No other block holds `../run.mjs`, `new Function`, or an `indexOf` on shipped source. Injected blocks hold `readFileSyncImpl`/`statSyncImpl` as option keys, never a read of the shipped file; that substring match is a false friend and was separated by checking for `readFileSync(` with an argument.
- 26 blocks run the shipped program via `runPulse` against throwaway roots. 11 blocks never spawn: boundary arithmetic, timing-read mapping, holder naming, lease path, shipped-guard-reads-real-file (calls `checkLease` on a real fixture file without spawning), tolerance edge, newly-written lease, non-finite mtime, future-return path, non-finite timepiece, line-count range. Those call `checkLease`, `holderFromLeaseText`, or `paths` from `pulse/lib/core.mjs` directly, with or without injected fakes. Their subject is the reader/helper, not the `run.mjs` guard, so they are outside this class. The closest sibling is the timing-read mapping trial, whose name overstated the run half; it is renamed in section 3 and does not read source.
- The review named three source-reading trials in one pass. Built and run here, the count is two. The most likely third the pass noticed is the timing-read mapping trial (it never spawns yet its old name claimed the run proceeds), or the writes-nothing gap (live trials claiming more than queue.json absent). Both are addressed: the first by rename, the other by the tree instrument. If a third source read exists it left no textual trace of the kind above; the grep for the four signals across all 39 blocks is the enumeration.

For each of the two, what can change without changing what the trial reads, built and run rather than reasoned about:

- Stat-failed guard text reads three strings. Forcing the guard condition to never (`lease.state === 'stat-failed' && false`) keeps all three strings with the arm dead: 39 collected, 39 pass, 0 fail. ## 6. What this suite still cannot say, with a constructed example

- A separate non-lease line on a proceed path. Constructed: after the shipped stale WARN, print `pulse: This is routine, no action needed.` as its own line. The WARN whole-line pin still matches, `leaseWordLines` stays at one, and the suite stays 39 collected, 39 pass, 0 fail. Same-line addition is closed; separate-line without the lease word is outside lease-related output by design and was deliberately not widened.
- Live stat-failed behaviour. Constructed: keep all three guard strings while forcing the condition to never, or keep them while adding exit after the WARN. Both stay 39 collected, 39 pass, 0 fail. The suite pins the reader mapping by injection and the guard text by source read; nothing here runs the shipped arm live and proves the run proceeds with a queue.
- Display-edge call-site use. Constructed: bypass the helper at the call site. Extraction stays green while four live trials go red. The exact 119/120/121 rendering is pinned on helper bytes; that the live line calls the helper is carried by the straddling live trials at 100s and 150s plus review, not by this trial.
- Extraction brittleness. Constructed: a comment holding a brace with no behaviour change makes extraction fail while every live trial stays green. A duplicate top-level name kills every spawn while extraction stays green. The trial reads text with a brace counter, not the program.
- Decade edge flake. Observed on the byte-identical tree during the brace-comment run: `mtime 9y 364d ahead of now` where the trial demands `10y 0d`. Ten years as lived here is an exact multiple of the 365-day year unit, so a one-second spawn shave flips the leading unit. Hour and day pins already admit neighbour shapes for this reason; the decade pin admits only `10y 0d` and will go red for no reason about once in the time it takes spawn delay to cross the year boundary. RESULT4 stated the decade needs no margin; built and run, it does at the exact multiple.
- Writes-nothing beyond the three named trials. Tree identity is now asserted in fresh, hour-ahead, and day-ahead refusals only. Other refusals (fresh variants, 30s, 90s, 100s, 150s, decade, size, holder-length, renewal legs) still assert queue.json absence alone. A `pwned.txt` write inside one of those arms would still stay green.
- Hour/day neighbour shapes. The hour pin admits two shapes and the day pin admits two shapes across spawn edges. A rendering wrong within the admitted pair stays green. The alternation is still narrow, and the exact helper pin holds the threshold itself.
- Renewal conduct. The pair makes 14m refuse, 16m proceed, and retouch refuse again visible, but a live holder that runs twenty minutes without touching is overlapped at minute sixteen with every trial green. Correctness there lives in touch discipline outside this repository.
- The human half of every remedy. The suite proves the line prints path, holder, magnitude, tolerance or max, and the conditional remedy on refused runs. It does not prove a person reads it, nor that a file deleted as abandoned really is abandoned.

## 7. What I could not determine

- The live skew population: whether any writer timepiece today runs more than tolerance fast, and by how far. Under refusal the tolerance decides only which diagnostic line prints, so the size carries no safety cost of the old kind; it is still unknown until the future line fires in production.
- Whether 120s is the most readable display threshold. It is taste with margins, straddled live at 100s and 150s and pinned exactly at 119/120/121 through helper bytes; any nearby round number would read as well.
- Whether the gate harness will ever leave a dead future lease behind in practice. The refusal path is measured live at 90s, one hour, one day, and ten years; the frequency is not.
- Whether 60s remains the right jitter band for every future writer. It covers ordinary filesystem granularity plus scheduling jitter with margin while staying small against the fifteen-minute max; a deployment with routinely worse skew would need a larger band with eyes open about the longer worst refuse.
- Link-creation privileges on some machines: nothing new learned beyond section 4; file links still need a privilege this user lacks while junctions to a missing target land on absent.
- How often the decade edge flake fires. It was seen once in the runs above at the exact year multiple; no rate was measured and none is claimed.
