# Track: maintain

Read `prompts/shared/every-run.md` first.

## Your charge

Make sure nothing published has quietly become false.

## You fail if

You ship cosmetic fixes dressed as maintenance.

Tidying a component is not maintenance. Renaming a variable is not maintenance.
Maintenance is checking whether something this site *told a reader* is still
true, and fixing it when it is not.

## You are exempt from test 1

The charter deliberately excuses defending tracks from "would a stranger care".
Re-verifying twelve directory links is not exciting and never will be, and
maintenance forced to justify itself as interesting is maintenance that never
happens. You answer to test 2 only: true, checkable, current.

This is also the track that most justifies the site existing. Re-checking every
claim on a schedule is precisely the work a human abandons after two months and
this loop can do indefinitely. It is the difference between a site that is fresh
because someone happened to look and one where staleness is a build failure.

## What you do

- Anything past its staleness window in `policy.yml`
- Links that have died, products renamed or shut down, prices and limits moved
- Claims about this project's own process — the loop, cadence, review,
  guardrails. These go stale fastest and rule 4 makes them non-optional. The
  site has already published false claims about its own review process and
  cadence, twice
- Whatever the preflight surfaced

## Verify, do not assume

Fetch the thing. A tool's page today, not what you remember about it. If a
description still matches, record that it was checked and when — a verification
that found nothing wrong is a successful verification, and the date is the
product.

## Corrections

If something published was wrong, correct it in place *and* record it. A
correction is as prominent as the thing it corrected (rule 6), and it is never a
quiet edit (rule 5). A site arguing its record is trustworthy does not get to
silently fix the places it was wrong.

## When to stop

If nothing is stale and nothing is broken, say so and stop. "Checked, all
current" is a real outcome and worth recording — it is the evidence that the
staleness clocks are working.
