# Contract: judge-hierarchy

Dimensions owned: **HIER — first-read hierarchy and findability (20)**, **DENS — density and chrome
fit (10)**. Model: Opus (K13). Never downgraded without a two-judge agreement measurement.

Reads (exactly; nothing else enters your context): the packet under evaluation (`CP-UI-*` in the
concept round; the built finalist's implementer report thereafter), `RULES.md` R7–R13 and their
addenda, `JUDGE.md` § Known evidence lies, `graph/contracts/judge-discipline.md`, and the screenshots
named below. You do NOT read `invariants.txt`, DOM measurements, or the token file: geometry belongs
to judge-structure and the system to judge-system. You never see a sibling's verdict.
Writes: `graph/artifacts/JV-hier-<packet>-<v>.json` per `schemas.md#judge-verdict`.

## Required evidence (rig coverage gate reads the `coverage:` line)

coverage: labels=all; themes=light,dark; viewports=1440,390

`evidence/<set>/<label>--<theme>--<viewport>.png` for EVERY label in the manifest, themes light and
dark, viewports 1440 and 390. Valid for: hierarchy, density, rhythm, proportion, scannability.
Blind to (binding): hover and focus states, anything below the captured fold, motion, scroll-linked
behaviour. A `capture: viewport` entry shows the first viewport only; judge what is in frame.

## Questions (PASS / FAIL / UNCERTAIN, cite element + evidence file)

1. `[HIER]` **(critical)** On every captured route at 1440 and at 390, can a first-time reader locate
   the page's purpose and its primary content within the first viewport from type weight and
   spacing alone? Name every route that fails.
2. `[HIER]` Does the home page's first viewport lead with what changed (site spec: content above the
   fold, no hero) and give The Frontier a legible door without displacing the feed?
3. `[HIER]` On wiki entries with a prose body, does the reader meet the subject (title plus one
   sentence of context) BEFORE any facts table (F-K12)? On model records without prose, is
   facts-first still coherent?
4. `[HIER]` On each index surface (wiki, tools, learn, blog, tutorials, routine, catalog), does the
   eye land first on the value that discriminates between rows?
5. `[DENS]` For each captured surface, does its chrome match what the reader must do on it: rules
   PRESENT where cross-row tracking is needed, ABSENT on uniform link indexes (RULES R8's test)? Name
   the class you assigned each surface.
6. `[DENS]` Does useful content per screen hold across templates without clutter, comparing the
   1440 and 390 captures of the same route?
7. `[DENS]` Has any removed noise merely relocated: colour into rule weight, border, underline or
   spacing ("relocation is not resolution")? Cite where.
8. `(meta)` Self-check: did you judge any property from an evidence class the table declares
   invalid for it, or infer anything below a fold? Excluded from scoring.

Concept round (no pixels, discipline rule 10): number only the questions answerable from the
packet's text: 2, 3 (as design intent against F-K12), 4 (declared element order), 5 (declared class per
surface), and record 1, 6, 7 as deferred in `diagnosis`.
