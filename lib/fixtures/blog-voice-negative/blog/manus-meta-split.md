If you use Manus, read this paragraph first. On 11
August 2026 Manus said it will “soon resume operating as an
independent company” after Meta’s acquisition of it. Part
of that transition deletes some users’ data, and the window to
back it up closes at 7:59 a.m. SGT on 23 August 2026
— that is 7:59 p.m. EDT on 22 August 2026, or 1:59 a.m. CEST on
23 August 2026, per Manus’s own conversion. Only data generated
on or
after 29 December 2025, by accounts Manus has identified as affected,
is in scope; unaffected accounts get an in-app notice and do nothing.
Manus says it will not charge affected users during the backup period
and will give them a welcome-back bonus once their data is restored.

## What an affected account needs to do, and by when

Now through 7:59 a.m. SGT, 23 August 2026 —
back up your data. Manus says it built backup and restoration tools
for this and has been sending reminders to affected accounts.

8:00 a.m., 23 August – 24 August 2026 (SGT)
— the affected data is deleted. Affected accounts lose access
for this two-day window.

From 8:00 a.m. SGT, 25 August 2026 — restore
opens for accounts that backed up.

Not sure if you’re affected? Manus points affected and
unaffected users to an

account-status guide in its Help Center

and says unaffected accounts receive an in-app notice rather than
email.

## Not a data breach — Manus’s own answer

The deletion reads, out of context, like an incident. Manus’s
FAQ addresses that directly: “Is this because of a data breach
or security incident? No. This measure stems from Manus transitioning
to independent operations and to comply with regulatory requirements.
It is not the result of any security incident.” Elsewhere the
FAQ gives the same reasoning in different words: “This is part
of our separation from Meta; we must take this step to comply with
regulatory requirements in specific parts of the world.” Manus
does not name the requirement or the jurisdiction, and this post does
not fill that gap with a guess.

## The split, and why data before 29 December 2025 is untouched

Manus’s own post frames the news as a return: “Manus will
soon return to operating as an independent company, which will see us
continue to serve our millions of users around the world.” It
does not, anywhere on the page, name the price of the deal it is
exiting or say why the deal ended — for that, this post relies
on reporting, not Manus. The Verge
reports that “Meta acquired the agentic AI startup for $2
billion last year and has already added some of Manus’ tools
into its platforms. But now, Manus has announced that it will
‘return to operating as an independent company’ after
China blocked the deal.” Both the $2 billion figure and the
China block are The Verge’s reporting (the piece links its own
earlier coverage of the block), attributed here as such because
Manus’s own announcement states neither.

The 29 December 2025 cutoff for affected data is Manus’s
explanation, not the Verge’s: its FAQ says “On December
29, 2025, Meta acquired Manus. Data generated by some users on/after
Meta’s acquisition of Manus needs to be deleted to comply”
with the regulatory requirement it cites but does not name. That date
is why the deletion is scoped to some data and some users rather than
the whole product: it marks when Meta’s ownership, and whatever
obligation now attaches to data generated under it, began.

## Not a shutdown

Nothing in Manus’s post says the product is closing. Unaffected
users, which the post implies is most of them, are told explicitly to
do nothing and keep using Manus “as usual.” The post ends
by saying the company has not slowed its release pace and is
“preparing a series of new features that will push the
boundaries of what’s possible for general AI agents once
again” — a forward-looking claim, made by Manus about
Manus, that this post is not in a position to verify and reports only
as what the company says.
